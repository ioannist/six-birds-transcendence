# S35 — Replacement-order critical pair

**Battery item:** 35 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** Two locally admissible replacement orders reach publicly identical endpoints but differ in hidden/package residue.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `route_confluence_stress_case`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S35`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Provide a minimal confluence and path-independence test for gradual transfer protocols.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- Two source components A and B can each be replaced by target components A' and B' using locally admissible steps.
- Route r1 replaces A then B; route r2 replaces B then A.
- Both routes yield the same declared public endpoint and carrier inventory.
- A predictive, memory, action-credit, or binding residue differs unless an additional commutation/confluence condition holds.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- Same endpoint inventory is not same transported object.
- Local admissibility is not confluence.
- Gradual replacement order is not automatically irrelevant.
- A path-independent public shadow can conceal holonomy.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
