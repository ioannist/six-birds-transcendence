# S24 — Many-to-many sharded reconfiguration

**Battery item:** 24 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** A distributed source assembly is repartitioned into a differently sharded target assembly.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `genuine_cross_carrier_distributed_case`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S24`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Test whether a candidate handles carrier boundaries, distributed package unity, and non-bijective component remapping without inventing a privileged central token.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- Multiple source shards jointly realize a declared package through explicit interfaces.
- Responsibility is redistributed across multiple target shards with different boundaries and replication factors.
- Some source and target shards overlap temporally, but no single shard contains the whole package.
- After reconfiguration, all source-specific routing and coordination services are withdrawn.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 5. Controlled variants

- replication before deletion
- erasure-coded shards
- network partition during cutover
- different shard counts

A candidate may be sensitive to a variant, but it must name the sensitivity rather than silently changing the scenario.

## 7. Scenario-specific stress questions

- What is the carrier token when no component is individually package-complete?
- Do pairwise shard matches glue into one target package?
- Can replication create hidden branch multiplicity or double action credit?
- What remains after legacy coordination is withdrawn?

## 10. Blocked inferences

- A shard is not automatically a subject token.
- Pairwise consistency is not global binding.
- Replication is not branch-valued lineage by itself.
- Distributed persistence is not a central-object migration narrative.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
