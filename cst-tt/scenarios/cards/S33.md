# S33 — Dormant backup activated after source erasure

**Battery item:** 33 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** A target backup is created while the source lives, remains inert for a long interval, and activates only after source erasure.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `genuine_cross_carrier_stale_backup_case`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S33`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Distinguish creation time, activation time, causal provenance, temporal gap, and the possibility of a dormant but nonoperating target structure.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- A disjoint target backup is written from the source and then kept dynamically inert.
- The source continues and acquires new history not included in the backup.
- The source is later erased; after another interval the old backup is activated.
- The activated target exactly matches the source only at backup time and is otherwise historically stale.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 5. Controlled variants

- short versus long staleness
- periodic incremental updates
- source knowingly authorizes backup
- multiple dormant backups

A candidate may be sensitive to a variant, but it must name the sensitivity rather than silently changing the scenario.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- A backup is not an active target episode by definition.
- Later activation does not retroactively include missing history.
- Source erasure does not update a stale record.
- Snapshot fidelity is time-indexed.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
