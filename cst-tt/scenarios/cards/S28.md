# S28 — Bridge removal followed by delayed target collapse

**Battery item:** 28 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** A target appears stable after handoff but fails only after a long bridge-free interval.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `autonomy_horizon_failure_control`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S28`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Force autonomy claims to state horizon, disturbance set, resource accounting, and delayed dependence rather than using instantaneous cutover success.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- A target operates with a temporary source/bridge during acquisition and an initial stabilization period.
- The bridge is removed at time w, after which all declared public tests pass for a finite interval.
- At a later time the target leaves its viability/package region because a slowly depleted resource or unrefreshed structure was not target-native.
- The delayed dependency is not visible to the ordinary short-horizon instruments.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- Immediate post-cutover success is not autonomy.
- Finite observation cannot establish indefinite maintenance without a theorem.
- Delayed collapse does not retroactively erase all structural transport.
- Public stability is not a viability invariant.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
