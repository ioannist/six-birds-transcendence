# S21 — Checkpoint/restart while the source is paused

**Battery item:** 21 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** A recoverable source is paused, a checkpoint activates a target, and the source remains available for possible resumption.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `genuine_cross_carrier_recoverable_source_case`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S21`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Separate temporary dynamical suspension from erasure, copying, and a genuinely empty interval with no recoverable source episode.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- The active source dynamics are paused after a checkpoint is produced; the source support and checkpoint remain recoverable.
- A disjoint target is activated from the checkpoint after a nonzero interval.
- The source can in principle be resumed, but no simultaneous source/target execution is stipulated in the base case.
- Checkpoint fidelity is exact only at the declared predictive-state interface.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 7. Scenario-specific stress questions

- Does a paused recoverable source create branching even before it is resumed?
- Which facts distinguish a temporal gap from continued dormant support?
- Can target autonomy be tested without destroying the checkpoint or source?
- Does restart preserve the source clock, reset it, or require a noncanonical translation?

## 11. Nonclaims
