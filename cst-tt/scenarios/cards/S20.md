# S20 — Pure representation or gauge change on one carrier

**Battery item:** 20 (canonical)
**Plan wording:** A pure representation/gauge change that should not count as movement to a new carrier.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `same_carrier_negative_control`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S20`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Provide the carrier-distinctness negative control and detect criteria that manufacture migration by relabeling one fixed support.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- One support, dynamics, resource law, and implementation map are retained.
- Only coordinates, labels, gauge, serialization, or presentation change through an exactly invertible translation.
- No separately typed target implementation or support is introduced.
- Every named package structure is preserved under the representation isomorphism.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- A new representation is not a new carrier by declaration alone.
- Exact isomorphism is not evidence of substrate independence.
- Same-carrier persistence is not cross-carrier transport.
- The control does not deny that representation changes can be scientifically useful.

## 11. Nonclaims
