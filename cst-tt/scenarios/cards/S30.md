# S30 — Certificate-only migration with package mismatch

**Battery item:** 30 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** A target reproduces the source certificate and audit outputs while the underlying package differs.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `certificate_shadow_negative_realization_control`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S30`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Prevent certificate copying, report matching, or verifier acceptance from standing in for realization or certificate soundness.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- A source emits a finite structural certificate and associated public audit outputs.
- A disjoint target stores/replays an exact copy of that certificate and passes the same certificate-level verifier.
- The target's underlying dynamics are stipulated to lack at least one package clause required by the certificate's intended interpretation.
- No certificate-soundness theorem is assumed.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- Certificate equality is not package equality.
- Verifier acceptance is not certificate soundness.
- An honest source certificate does not transfer its referent by copying.
- Auditability is not ontology.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
