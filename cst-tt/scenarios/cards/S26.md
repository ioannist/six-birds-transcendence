# S26 — Independently built package-equivalent target

**Battery item:** 26 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** A target is built independently to be package-equivalent, with no causal bridge from the designated source token.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `independent_realization_negative_lineage_control`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S26`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Separate structural realization/equivalence from source-derived transport and lineage.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- A source and a disjoint target are independently constructed from a common specification, not from one another.
- They are stipulated package-equivalent under a declared isomorphism and receive matched initial environments.
- There is no source-to-target causal or informational route beyond the common design specification.
- Both operate autonomously from activation.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.
