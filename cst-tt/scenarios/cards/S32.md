# S32 — Approximately reversible round trip with accumulated residual

**Battery item:** 32 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** Source-to-target-to-source transport nearly recovers the start, but repeated cycles accumulate a structured defect.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `approximate_composition_stress_case`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S32`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Separate one-cycle closeness from stable approximate transport, flatness, and long-horizon package viability.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- A disjoint target is reached by an approximate bridge and returned by an approximate inverse.
- One round trip stays within a declared defect bound.
- Repeated cycles accumulate a directional residual in a named predictive or memory coordinate.
- Public outputs remain within tolerance for more cycles than the hidden/package coordinate.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- One-cycle closeness is not stable approximation.
- Approximate inverse is not flat transport.
- Public tolerance does not bound hidden drift.
- A defect bound without a composition law is incomplete.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
