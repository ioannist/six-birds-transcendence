# S23 — Many-source fusion into one target

**Battery item:** 23 (supplemental stress control)
**Plan wording:** Two independently developed source episodes are combined into one target package.
**Cross-carrier/control status:** `genuine_cross_carrier_fusion_case`
**Outcome vector:** deliberately unfilled (`S-T=UNFILLED; S-R=UNFILLED; S-L=UNFILLED; A=UNFILLED; R=UNFILLED`)
**Machine records:** `scenarios/PROTOCOL_AXIS_MATRIX.csv` and `ledgers/scenario_discriminator_matrix.csv`, keyed by `S23`

## 1. Discriminating purpose

Provide a pure many-to-one case in which history conflict, recombination, and action-credit cannot be reduced to copying one source.

## 2. Stipulated scenario facts

- Two disjoint active sources have distinct predictive histories and partially incompatible ledgers.
- A fusion protocol produces one target package on a third support.
- The target is stipulated to answer a declared union of public queries from both sources.
- Both sources are erased after target activation; full predictive and package coherence are not stipulated.

These facts define the case. They do not preassign transport, target-package, lineage, autonomy, recognition, or ontological outputs.

## 10. Blocked inferences

- Public union is not history union.
- Many-to-one engineering topology is not fusion lineage by definition.
- Conflict resolution can destroy information while preserving reports.
- Equal source labels do not remove asymmetric causal histories.

## 11. Nonclaims

This card does not say whether a conscious subject moves, survives, dies, branches, fuses, or is recreated. It does not assert that the source or target is conscious. It defines an operational stress case for later structural candidates.
