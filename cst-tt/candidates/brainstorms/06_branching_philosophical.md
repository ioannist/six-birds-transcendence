# Branching/philosophical formal pass

**Status:** independent CST-EXP-03 brainstorming pass; not a synthesis, ranking, or target recommendation.

**Lens:** fission, fusion, duplication, identity demarcation  
**Scenario families to challenge:** S03;S05;S09;S10;S23;S26

## Starting point

Prefer a relation-valued structural answer over an arbitrary unique continuer, and state exactly where personal identity remains an extra interpretation.

## Ideas generated within this lens

1. Use symmetry to prove nonselection in exact duplication.
2. Represent one-to-many and many-to-one ancestry without forcing numerical identity.
3. Preserve shared prefixes and nonretroactivity under later deletion.
4. Require explicit reconciliation for fusion of conflicting histories.
5. Seek a valuable structural classification/no-go even if no positive personal-identity target survives.

## Internal tension

A relation can become too permissive; structural ancestry may not answer the motivating subjective question.

## Translation discipline

Every retained idea must later be represented using separate source/target carrier types, a bridge datum, a lane-typed verdict vector, explicit assumptions, and a nonclaim boundary. Metaphors such as persistence, transfer, or self are not formal primitives.

## Phase boundary

This memo does not assert that its lens is necessary or sufficient, and it does not settle consciousness, survival, or personal identity.
