# Dynamical/process pass

**Status:** independent CST-EXP-03 brainstorming pass; not a synthesis, ranking, or target recommendation.

**Lens:** ongoing causal organization, moving support, handoff intervals  
**Scenario families to challenge:** S01;S02;S06;S07;S21;S22;S27

## Starting point

Ask whether continuation is a property of an ongoing process that changes support, rather than any equivalence relation between endpoint states.

## Ideas generated within this lens

1. Model replacement as one changing-support history with a responsibility map.
2. Distinguish a continuous fork from a detached copy reconstructed from a record.
3. Treat temporal gaps as explicit failures of the strict process criterion, not universal no-gos.
4. Require a process invariant through every intermediate stage, including mixed-support stages.
5. Use source withdrawal to separate takeover from indefinitely coupled co-processing.

## Internal tension

Overlap can be arbitrary or transmit only a public shadow; a strict no-gap rule may exclude plausible checkpoint cases.

## Translation discipline

Every retained idea must later be represented using separate source/target carrier types, a bridge datum, a lane-typed verdict vector, explicit assumptions, and a nonclaim boundary. Metaphors such as persistence, transfer, or self are not formal primitives.

## Phase boundary

This memo does not assert that its lens is necessary or sufficient, and it does not settle consciousness, survival, or personal identity.
