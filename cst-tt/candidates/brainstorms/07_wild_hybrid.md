# Wild hybrid pass

**Status:** independent CST-EXP-03 brainstorming pass; not a synthesis, ranking, or target recommendation.

**Lens:** minimal multi-layer bundle with defect currency  
**Scenario families to challenge:** S11;S12;S15;S16;S29;S32;S35

## Starting point

Construct the smallest counterexample-complete bundle rather than beginning from either the full C-package or one privileged invariant.

## Ideas generated within this lens

1. Start with predictive dynamics, then add only structures forced by counterexamples: route residue, credit/clock history, glue, and viability margin.
2. Treat missing distinctions as a priced adequacy residual rather than silently expanding the package.
3. Allow different bridge morphologies for different layers but require one compatibility diagram.
4. Search for incomparability between the resulting bundle and the simplified/full certificates.
5. Keep a no-go fallback: no finite observable bundle uniquely determines token identity under symmetry.

## Internal tension

Minimality can be post hoc; multi-layer bridges may be harder to compose than the full package they were meant to simplify.

## Translation discipline

Every retained idea must later be represented using separate source/target carrier types, a bridge datum, a lane-typed verdict vector, explicit assumptions, and a nonclaim boundary. Metaphors such as persistence, transfer, or self are not formal primitives.

## Phase boundary

This memo does not assert that its lens is necessary or sufficient, and it does not settle consciousness, survival, or personal identity.
