# TS-01 — Predictive-Quotient Bisimulation

**Status:** exploratory candidate schema; equal phase status; unranked; not selected.  
**Lens:** quotient/algebraic  
**Primary claim lane:** S-T  
**Candidate version:** v1

## One-sentence idea

A cross-carrier episode preserves the predictive object when separately typed future-sufficient quotient dynamics are related by an admissible bisimulation or conjugacy.

## Formal objects and types

- **Source object:** `PredSys(X_S) = (Q_S,U_S,delta_S,admiss_S)`
- **Target object:** `PredSys(X_T) = (Q_T,U_T,delta_T,admiss_T)`
- **Common comparison type / relation:** A predictive comparison object Q_* with typed legs e_S:Q_S->Q_* and e_T:Q_T->Q_* or a relation R subset Q_S x Q_T.
- **Transported-object atlas IDs:** TO-02;TO-03;TO-04
- **Invariant IDs:** IV-01;IV-02;IV-03;IV-07
- **Bridge IDs:** BR-04;BR-05
- **Bridge signature:** (R,Phi_U,Phi_tau) where R is total on reachable source/target predictive classes and matches admissible continuation steps.
- **Bridge morphology:** bisimulation/conjugacy, optionally presented as a span through a common predictive quotient
- **Bridge data:** reachable predictive classes; continuation translators; clock/order relation; route witness; optional defect metric
- **Admissibility:** Future tests are declared independently of desired verdicts; R respects reachable transitions in both directions; carrier tokens are independently individuated.

## Exact and approximate versions

- **Exact preservation:** predictive class, admissible continuation transition, and selected loop/route action
- **Permitted variation:** microphysical implementation, public encoding, gauge, and nonpredictive package features not claimed by this schema
- **Approximate/noisy version:** An epsilon-bisimulation metric on predictive transition systems with a declared horizon and composition law.

## Carrier, support, and clocks

- **Source status:** Stipulated finite predictive system; no actual conscious source is inherited.
- **Target status:** Goal is a target-native predictive system related by R; full-package status remains separate.
- **Support through stages:** Q_S is carried by X_S; bridge records live on B_ST; Q_T must be generated by X_T after withdrawal.
- **Clock/order structure:** Local clocks are related by a declared monotone translator; no universal now is assumed.

## Preservation audit

- **Hiddenness/public matching:** Public equivalence is insufficient; privileged continuation tests or complete-state quotient construction are required in toys.
- **Action credit/provenance:** Not preserved unless explicitly added; a major boundary of the schema.
- **Temporal closure:** Only the order needed for predictive transitions is preserved; package-minted temporal closure is not implied.
- **Recombination/global structure:** Branchwise quotient preservation is not enough when recombination probes distinguish routes; such probes must enter the test family.
- **Viability/maintenance:** Not intrinsic to the core schema; target autonomy and viability are separately audited.

## Route, composition, branching, and deletion

- **Route policy:** Routes may differ; route independence is claimed only after a flatness/confluence or zero-holonomy proof on the predictive object.
- **Loop/holonomy test:** Transport a predictive class around each generated route loop and compare the returned class/action.
- **Composition:** Compose typed bisimulations only when continuation and clock translators align; retain accumulated route defect.
- **Copy:** Every qualifying copy is a structural branch; no unique target is selected by quotient equivalence.
- **Fission:** Return branch-valued continuation for every future-sufficient branch satisfying the relation.
- **Fusion:** Return fusion-valued ancestry only when a target predictive class lawfully combines all declared predecessors.
- **Deletion:** Source deletion does not change a previously computed structural relation.
- **Empty target:** No reachable target predictive class is related under the admissible bridge.

## Controls and possible later theorem forms

- **Positive control:** S18 exact reversible round trip on the declared predictive package.
- **Negative control:** S11 public-perfect copy with predictive mismatch.
- **Strong adversarial case:** S12 predictive match with different binding/action-credit shows the schema's package incompleteness.
- **Possible theorem shapes:** Existence/classification of heterogeneous predictive bisimulations; composition under aligned translators; flatness implies route-independent predictive image.
- **Possible no-go/boundary shapes:** Public/current equivalence cannot determine predictive transport; symmetric quotient-isomorphic copies cannot yield unique lineage without asymmetry.
- **Toy discriminators:** two nonisomorphic finite automata with isomorphic predictive quotients; public-perfect/future-different pair; two-route holonomy automaton; symmetric duplicate.
- **Smallest reconnaissance model:** Two 3–5 state automata on disjoint carrier types with one public quotient, one predictive refinement, and two bridge routes.
- **Minimal dependencies:** finite sets, relations, transition tables, quotient computation, route enumeration; no Six Birds simulation

## Falsification and kill criteria

- **Direct falsifier/downgrade test:** A finite bridge satisfying the proposed bisimulation equations but failing an admitted continuation test, or composition that violates the claimed defect law.
- **Kill criteria:** Admissible test family is circular; cross-carrier comparison collapses to label isomorphism; no nontrivial composition law survives.
- **Nearest alternative:** TS-10 on discriminators S12;S18;S26;S35.
