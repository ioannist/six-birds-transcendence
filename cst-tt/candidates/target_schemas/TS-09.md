# TS-09 — Residual-Bounded Predictive Simulation Bundle

**Status:** exploratory candidate schema; equal phase status; unranked; not selected.  
**Lens:** approximation/minimal bundle  
**Primary claim lane:** S-T  
**Candidate version:** v1

## One-sentence idea

A target carries a deliberately minimal predictive–historical–viability bundle when an explicit vector residual remains below justified margins under composition and withdrawal.

## Formal objects and types

- **Source object:** `Bundle(X_S)=(Pred_S,Hist_S,VK_S,Gates_S)`
- **Target object:** `Bundle(X_T)`
- **Common comparison type / relation:** A probe-indexed bundle specification with vector metric and hard gates.
- **Transported-object atlas IDs:** TO-03;TO-06;TO-15
- **Invariant IDs:** IV-02;IV-06;IV-10
- **Bridge IDs:** BR-03;BR-11
- **Bridge signature:** B_epsilon with Delta=(d_pred,d_hist,d_glue,d_viab,d_auto) and declared margins/thresholds.
- **Bridge morphology:** residual-bounded forward simulation with hard-gate vector
- **Bridge data:** probe family; predictive transitions; selected historical/gluing tests; viability margins; defect composition law
- **Admissibility:** Features and thresholds are fixed from counterexamples or stability margins, not desired scenario labels; omitted dimensions are explicit.

## Exact and approximate versions

- **Exact preservation:** The zero-residual finite core and every declared hard gate
- **Permitted variation:** bounded deviations in soft coordinates within a stated horizon and perturbation family
- **Approximate/noisy version:** Defining form: Delta<=epsilon componentwise, epsilon below named stability/viability margins.

## Carrier, support, and clocks

- **Source status:** Finite bundle stipulated and fully observable in toy models.
- **Target status:** Target must satisfy the residual and hard-gate contract; autonomy is separately tested.
- **Support through stages:** Bundle components name their support and may migrate at different stages; a global compatibility gate is retained.
- **Clock/order structure:** Time reparameterization contributes a temporal residual rather than being silently ignored.

## Preservation audit

- **Hiddenness/public matching:** Probe completeness is a declared limitation; public-only probes cannot close the hidden residual.
- **Action credit/provenance:** Included as a selected hard or soft coordinate when the candidate uses it; omission is visible.
- **Temporal closure:** Represented by selected history/clock tests and a hard external-scheduler control.
- **Recombination/global structure:** Included through a gluing/recombination residual when branch scenarios are in scope.
- **Viability/maintenance:** Margin to viability boundary is explicit and controls approximation.

## Route, composition, branching, and deletion

- **Route policy:** Route defects accumulate by a declared nonlinear law; route-independent labels require all relevant loop defects below margin.
- **Loop/holonomy test:** Measure vector residual after closed routes and compare each hard-gate coordinate rather than one average fidelity.
- **Composition:** Compose simulations with an explicit bound F(Delta_1,Delta_2); reject if any hard gate or margin is crossed.
- **Copy:** Every qualifying source-derived copy can be a branch; small defect never selects a unique person.
- **Fission:** Branch-valued while every branch remains inside the declared bundle margins.
- **Fusion:** Fusion-valued or underdetermined depending on whether histories/gates admit one bounded merged bundle.
- **Deletion:** Deletion does not lower residual or create lineage.
- **Empty target:** No target state/history satisfies all hard gates and componentwise margins.

## Controls and possible later theorem forms

- **Positive control:** S17 or S25 inside a proved error/viability margin.
- **Negative control:** S16 at a threshold crossing and S30 certificate-only mismatch.
- **Strong adversarial case:** S32 approximately reversible round trip with accumulated residual.
- **Possible theorem shapes:** Finite exact core; perturbation/stability theorem away from boundaries; defect-composition theorem; counterexample-driven minimality theorem.
- **Possible no-go/boundary shapes:** Scalar fidelity cannot guarantee every critical gate; no uniform approximation theorem holds at zero viability/branch margin.
- **Toy discriminators:** finite bundle with predictive, ledger, glue, and viability bits; noisy bridge; high-average hard-gate counterfeit; two-stage residual accumulation.
- **Smallest reconnaissance model:** A pair of 4–8 state systems with four defect coordinates, one hard gate, one viability margin, and two composable bridges.
- **Minimal dependencies:** finite transition systems, vector metrics, exhaustive counterexample search, simple optimization

## Falsification and kill criteria

- **Direct falsifier/downgrade test:** A below-threshold bridge crosses a declared hard gate or a purported composition bound is violated by a finite counterexample.
- **Kill criteria:** Bundle selection is ad hoc; no stable margins exist; defect law grows too fast for any nontrivial multi-stage bridge.
- **Nearest alternative:** TS-04 on discriminators S02;S18;S24;S32.
