# TS-08 — Reflexive Temporal–Credit Ledger Bridge

**Status:** exploratory candidate schema; equal phase status; unranked; not selected.  
**Lens:** self/temporal history  
**Primary claim lane:** S-L  
**Candidate version:** v1

## One-sentence idea

Continuation preserves a source-specific reflexive self-coordinate, package-minted temporal order, and action-credit ledger through a typed clock/provenance bridge, allowing branches but rejecting externally scripted replicas.

## Formal objects and types

- **Source object:** `LedgerHist(X_S)=(sigma_S,E_S,prec_S,tau_S,credit_S)`
- **Target object:** `LedgerHist(X_T)`
- **Common comparison type / relation:** A history/ledger comparison object with clock connection and provenance morphisms.
- **Transported-object atlas IDs:** TO-07;TO-08;TO-10
- **Invariant IDs:** IV-12;IV-05
- **Bridge IDs:** BR-02;BR-08
- **Bridge signature:** B_L=(L_hist,phi_clock,phi_self,phi_credit) satisfying temporal/self/credit commutative diagrams.
- **Bridge morphology:** history relation plus clock/provenance functor
- **Bridge data:** self-coordinate histories; event order; local clocks; action-credit records; minting authorities; branch prefixes
- **Admissibility:** Clock and ledger mappings are nonretroactive, symmetry-respecting, and distinguish copied labels from endogenous updates.

## Exact and approximate versions

- **Exact preservation:** self-update relation, temporal order up to admitted reparameterization, credit provenance, and shared-prefix branch history
- **Permitted variation:** clock rate, implementation, representation, and post-branch new credit events
- **Approximate/noisy version:** Vector defect for phase/order, self-update, credit provenance, and ledger reconciliation, with hard nonretroactivity gates.

## Carrier, support, and clocks

- **Source status:** Finite ledger history stipulated; no metaphysical self is assumed.
- **Target status:** Target must generate new order, self-updates, and credit under target authority after withdrawal.
- **Support through stages:** Ledger records may be mirrored during overlap; minting authority and active update support are named at each stage.
- **Clock/order structure:** Monotone reparameterization is allowed; external phase service is not target-native closure.

## Preservation audit

- **Hiddenness/public matching:** Ledger and self-update structure may be privileged and can differ behind identical reports.
- **Action credit/provenance:** Defining component.
- **Temporal closure:** Defining component.
- **Recombination/global structure:** Shared-prefix branches and any fusion reconciliation are explicit.
- **Viability/maintenance:** Not core, but withdrawal may expose inability to mint/update the ledger.

## Route, composition, branching, and deletion

- **Route policy:** Order strongly matters; route independence is not expected when replacement order changes credit or phase history.
- **Loop/holonomy test:** Round-trip the ledger and compare phase, minted order, self-coordinate, and credit ancestry.
- **Composition:** Compose only when clock maps are monotone and ledger authorities/provenance align; retain branch prefixes.
- **Copy:** Copies share a prefix and become distinct ledger branches; no branch is uniquely selected by later deletion.
- **Fission:** Branch-valued with a common prefix and branch-local subsequent credit.
- **Fusion:** Fusion is underdetermined or blocked unless conflicting self/credit histories admit an explicit reconciliation law.
- **Deletion:** Deletion removes a branch but cannot alter prior shared-prefix classification.
- **Empty target:** No target history carries an admissible self/clock/credit continuation from the source prefix.

## Controls and possible later theorem forms

- **Positive control:** S19 with a lawful monotone clock translation and target-native ledger updates.
- **Negative control:** S29 external scheduler masquerading as temporal closure.
- **Strong adversarial case:** S10/S23 fusion of conflicting credit histories.
- **Possible theorem shapes:** Ledger-preservation theorem; clock-reparameterization invariance; nonretroactivity under deletion; fusion-reconciliation classification.
- **Possible no-go/boundary shapes:** Endpoint copies and external schedulers cannot establish target-native temporal/credit continuation.
- **Toy discriminators:** finite ledgers with branch prefixes; radical clock rescaling; external scheduler counterfeit; stale backup; conflicting fusion records.
- **Smallest reconnaissance model:** Two finite event/ledger systems with a clock rescaling, one branching prefix, one external scheduler, and one stale checkpoint.
- **Minimal dependencies:** finite partial orders, ledger provenance, monotone maps, branch/fusion checks

## Falsification and kill criteria

- **Direct falsifier/downgrade test:** The bridge accepts a target whose credit is minted by the source/scheduler or violates monotone/nonretroactive ledger constraints.
- **Kill criteria:** Ledger variables are only copied labels; provenance cannot be defined noncircularly; clock translation is arbitrary enough to trivialize tests.
- **Nearest alternative:** TS-03 on discriminators S06;S12;S19;S29;S33.
