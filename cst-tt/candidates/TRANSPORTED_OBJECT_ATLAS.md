## Detailed cards

### TO-01 — Integrated C1–C8 package

- **Type skeleton:** `CPkg(X) := (C1_X,...,C8_X, coherence_X, gates_X)`
- **Ontic/interface status:** intrinsic plus interface-relative, history-dependent, and maintained.
- **Corpus assets:** A-CT-01;A-PR-01;A-EX-01;A-EV-01;A-LG-01.
- **Candidate use:** TS-02.
- **What it offers:** Keeps the richest consciousness-relevant target contract in view.
- **Failure surface:** No integrated source or target instance is inherited; separate clause witnesses do not glue automatically.
- **Heterogeneous-carrier obligation:** Needs a heterogeneous package morphism and a target-native coherence re-audit.

### TO-02 — Terminal predictive quotient

- **Type skeleton:** `Q_X := Reach(X)/~_future,X`
- **Ontic/interface status:** intrinsic relative to an admissible continuation family; interface-relative.
- **Corpus assets:** A-PQ-01;A-PQ-02;A-RT-01.
- **Candidate use:** TS-01;TS-10.
- **What it offers:** Separates current/public matching from future-sufficient organization.
- **Failure surface:** Quotient isomorphism does not preserve full package, hidden implementation, token identity, or autonomy.
- **Heterogeneous-carrier obligation:** Needs separately typed continuation alphabets and a comparison of reachable predictive fibers.

### TO-03 — Predictive transition system

- **Type skeleton:** `Dyn_X := (Q_X, U_X, delta_X, admiss_X)`
- **Ontic/interface status:** history-sensitive and protocol-relative through admissible continuations.
- **Corpus assets:** A-PQ-01;A-PQ-02;A-RT-01;A-HO-01.
- **Candidate use:** TS-01;TS-09.
- **What it offers:** Adds dynamics and admissible futures to quotient-state matching.
- **Failure surface:** A matched transition graph can still omit binding, credit, temporal minting, and viability.
- **Heterogeneous-carrier obligation:** Needs input/clock translation and a proof that the bridge respects continuation semantics.

### TO-04 — Continuation algebra

- **Type skeleton:** `Cont_X := (Tests_X, compose_X, eval_X)`

- **Corpus assets:** A-RT-01;A-PQ-02;A-HO-01.

- **Heterogeneous-carrier obligation:** Needs noncircular admissible-test translation and composition across bridge stages.

### TO-05 — Closure operator and fixed point

- **Type skeleton:** `(L_X, Cl_X, F_X) with Cl_X(F_X)=F_X`
- **Ontic/interface status:** intrinsic to a declared law basis but realization-relative.
- **Corpus assets:** A-AOR-01;A-CT-01;A-EX-01;A-LG-01.
- **Candidate use:** TS-06.
- **What it offers:** Turns promoted package realization into a commuting closure/fixed-point question.
- **Failure surface:** A common fixed point can be shared by independent realizations and need not carry lineage.
- **Heterogeneous-carrier obligation:** Needs embeddings or a span that commute with closure and preserve globally glued witnesses.

## Cross-object observations

1. No object automatically supplies all five structural/recognition lanes. A predictive quotient can be transported while a full package, lineage, or autonomy fails; a target package can exist without source ancestry; a maintained target can be independent.
2. Objects divide into snapshot-like, dynamical, history/relation-valued, global/glued, and actively maintained families. The bridge morphology must match the family rather than being chosen for mathematical prestige.
3. Objects with public or interface-relative definitions require a declared interface and may change when the interface expands. Objects with maintenance status require a withdrawal boundary and horizon.
4. TO-15 is not a preferred synthesis. It is a controlled possibility whose components must later be justified by counterexamples or minimality results.
