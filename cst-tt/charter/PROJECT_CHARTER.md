# Project Charter: CST-EXP

**Phase:** CST-EXP-00  
**Charter status:** frozen for the preparatory exploration; amend only through a logged decision  
**Research posture:** target-neutral

## Mission

The project will explore whether a consciousness-relevant Six Birds package can be related across genuinely distinct carriers in a mathematically precise way, and which theorem-and-toy target is most defensible to attempt later. “Consciousness-relevant” here means only that the package is built from the structural clauses and dependencies used by the attached consciousness work. It does **not** mean that the package has been proved sufficient for consciousness, realized by an actual system, or preserved across media.

The project’s immediate output is not an answer. It is an informed target-selection dossier built by extraction, comparison, counterexample design, assumption pricing, and feasibility analysis.

## Neutral formal question

Given separately declared source and target carrier specifications `Σ_S` and `Σ_T`, a source package `P_S`, a target candidate `P_T`, and declared cross-carrier bridge/route data `B, ρ`, determine which of the following predicates can be coherently defined and plausibly studied:

- `Transport_B(P_S, P_T)` — a specified mathematical object is transported or preserved;
- `TargetPackage_C(Σ_T, P_T)` — the target structurally instantiates the relevant C-package;
- `Lineage_κ(P_S, P_T; ρ)` — the target stands in a declared continuation relation `κ` to the source;
- `Autonomous_h(P_T | remove(B, Σ_S, external protocol))` — the target maintains the specified package for a declared horizon or closure condition after handoff support is removed;
- `Recognized_E(P_T)` — declared empirical evidence is proposed to correspond to the structure; and
- `Ontic_Ω(P_T)` — an ontological or phenomenal reading follows only under an explicit assumption set `Ω`.

No predicate in this list implies another unless a separately tagged claim states and supports the implication.

## Questions kept open by charter

The exploration does not decide in advance:

- whether the transported item is a state, history, process, quotient, relation, certificate, closure fixed point, viability kernel, logical object, or package of these;
- whether the bridge is a function, relation, span, simulation, bisimulation, common refinement, recovery process, overlap process, or another typed object;
- whether lineage is one-to-one, relation-valued, branch-valued, path-dependent, partial, graded, or underdetermined;
- whether a positive result is exact, approximate, constructive, classificatory, conditional, or impossible under the permitted observations;
- whether gradual replacement, destructive copying, live migration, fission, fusion, dormant reconstruction, or distributed realization is the decisive scenario; or
- whether the strongest later landing is a positive theorem, a no-go theorem, an underdetermination theorem, or a mixed package.

## Independent inquiry axes

### 1. Structural transport (`S-T`)

What typed object crosses from `Σ_S` to `Σ_T`, under what bridge, and with what exact or approximate preservation law?

### 2. Target structural realization (`S-R`)

Does `P_T` satisfy the declared structural clauses and coherence gates on `Σ_T`, rather than only reproduce a selected output or public shadow?

### 3. Lineage (`S-L`)

What relation, if any, connects the source package/history to the target package/history? How does the relation classify duplication, fission, fusion, source persistence, deletion, route dependence, and an empty target?

### 4. Post-handoff autonomy (`A`)

After removing the source, bridge, decoder, or external protocol according to a declared intervention, does the target maintain the package endogenously?

### 5. Recognition (`R`)

Which observations could count as evidence for the structural predicates, with what false-positive, false-negative, identifiability, and intervention limits?

### 6. Ontological interpretation (`O`)

Which conclusions require certificate soundness, A1/Ontic Closure Completeness, A2/Substrate Indifference, or a narrower replacement premise? These assumptions must remain visible and independently rejectable.

## Permitted work

The preparatory phases may extract definitions and theorems, build provenance and assumption ledgers, formulate neutral candidate schemas, design finite thought-models and later toy-program contracts, create counterexamples and no-go conjectures, compare alternatives, research relevant literature at the designated phase, and rank targets. They may write conjecture-level statements and proof-spine sketches, but may not report them as landed results.

## Prohibited outcomes during exploration

The exploration must not certify a conscious system, identify a target as the same person, treat public equivalence as predictive or package equivalence, use A2 as a proved transport theorem, turn fixed-support promotion into cross-carrier migration by relabeling, or smuggle ontological language into structural premises.

## Decision standard

A later target is eligible only if it is genuinely cross-carrier, explicitly typed, noncircular, adversarially discriminating, finite-model accessible, source-traceable, and clear about assumptions and nonclaims. The project is successful even if the best-supported target is negative or underdetermination-based.

## Phase-00 success condition

CST-EXP-00 is complete when central terms are nonconclusory, all claims can be typed into separate lanes, the principal questions can be stated independently, and the question/anti-conflation ledgers are usable without relying on conversational context.
