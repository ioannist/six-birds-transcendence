# Non-Goals and Scope Guards

## 1. Phase-wide non-goals

The preparatory program is not authorized to:

1. prove that consciousness can or cannot move between substrates;
2. select a preferred transfer criterion before the candidate and scenario atlases are complete;
3. implement Python toys, Lean proofs, simulations, migration systems, or empirical protocols;
4. run a full Six Birds simulation;
5. certify any biological, artificial, hybrid, simulated, or hypothetical system as conscious;
6. decide personal identity by stipulation;
7. treat the attached consciousness certificate as already sound or instantiated;
8. formalize A2 merely by quantifying a definition over an arbitrary carrier type;
9. use public behavior, reports, computation, or endpoint similarity as sufficient for hidden predictive organization;
10. turn a fixed-support promotion result into a cross-carrier result by renaming supports;
11. count alternate versions of one paper as independent evidence; or
12. use the owner-excluded paper identified in the normative plan.

## 2. Fail-closed scope guards

## 3. Source-handling guards

- The 60 manuscript files must all be accounted for during CST-EXP-01, including non-primary papers.
- Papers 42 and 60 are one versioned Strict Audited Utility line. The newer revision is normative unless a statement-level comparison requires both.
- The owner exclusion recorded in the plan remains controlling. This phase does not reinterpret or relax it.
- The corpus is mathematical/conceptual input, not empirical evidence that any actual system is conscious or transferable.
- Existing fixed-support theorems must carry a visible `fixed_support=true` flag in later ledgers.

## 4. Language policy

The following words may appear in titles, historical quotations, or informal motivation, but not as unanalysed formal conclusions:

`consciousness transfer`, `mind upload`, `same person`, `survival`, `transcendence`, `migration`, `copy`, `death`, `awakening`, `resurrection`, `substrate independence`.

A formal statement must replace them with one or more of the project predicates: `Transport`, `TargetPackage`, `Lineage`, `Autonomous`, `Recognition`, and `Ontic`.

## 5. Exploration-stage stop rules

A line of exploration must be paused or downgraded when:

- its definition contains the verdict it is meant to prove;
- every positive example passes only because the bridge hard-codes the target answer;
- it cannot distinguish an intended continuation from a public-perfect counterfeit;
- it has no coherent verdict for symmetric duplication;
- it depends on an undeclared external decoder or controller;
- it collapses under route composition or loop holonomy;
- it requires A1/A2 while being presented as structural;
- it cannot be instantiated in an exact finite model even in principle, unless the reason for requiring a nonfinite setting is itself the target; or
- it has no falsifier, negative control, or meaningful boundary.

A stopped candidate remains in the rejection ledger with the reason; it is not erased.
