# RT-03 — Duplication critic

## Strongest adversarial thesis

Any criterion that says an upload is uniquely me fails when an exactly symmetric second upload is added.

## Candidate/family hit

Every function-valued or uniqueness-seeking interpretation of TS-01 through TS-09.

## Concession the project should make

The symmetry objection is decisive against unpriced unique selection.

## Required response

Return a relation, branch-valued successor set, principled underdetermination, or an independently justified asymmetry.

## Remainder after response

Relation-valued lineage avoids contradiction but does not by itself answer first-person anticipation or numerical identity.

This review is intentionally adversarial but does not claim that its standpoint is correct.
