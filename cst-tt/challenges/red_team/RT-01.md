# RT-01 — Strict functionalist

## Strongest adversarial thesis

Exact preservation of the right causal organization should already be sufficient; extra lineage and biological continuity demands risk mystification.

## Candidate/family hit

TS-03 may overvalue overlap; TS-04 and TS-08 may duplicate functional organization under new labels.

## Concession the project should make

A structural project may legitimately seek organizational invariants and reject substrate chemistry as primitive.

## Required response

Specify exactly which organization is complete enough, how implementation is nonvacuous, and why duplication yields relation-valued rather than unique conclusions.

## Remainder after response

Organizational invariance remains a philosophical/psychophysical premise, not a theorem derived from SBT.

This review is intentionally adversarial but does not claim that its standpoint is correct.
