# RT-02 — Biological or dynamical continuity critic

## Strongest adversarial thesis

A translated abstract structure may be a replica; continuation may require the living, embodied, temporally continuous process itself.

## Candidate/family hit

TS-01, TS-02, TS-05, TS-06, TS-09, and TS-10 can succeed without live overlap.

## Concession the project should make

Process continuity must be specified without simply saying 'the same subject continues.'

## Required response

Offer a noncircular process invariant, model embodiment/environmental closure explicitly, and explain gaps, destructive copies, and gradual replacement.

## Remainder after response

SF-02 can represent this view, but no corpus theorem establishes that its process criterion is necessary for consciousness.

This review is intentionally adversarial but does not claim that its standpoint is correct.
