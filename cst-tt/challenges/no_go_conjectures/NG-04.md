# NG-04 — Branchwise preservation does not imply recombination preservation

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

Separate preservation of every branch marginal does not imply preservation of joint recombination information.

## Smallest intended witness

Matched branch states with different parity/correlation revealed only after recombination.

## Proposed proof spine

Use two joint distributions or automata with identical marginals and distinct joint probe values.

## Candidate impact

`TS-01;TS-03;TS-05;TS-08;TS-09;TS-10`

## Assumptions

joint probes admitted

## Falsifier or escape condition

A factorization theorem showing all joint probes are determined by branch marginals.

## Nonclaim

Does not require quantum structure; a finite classical witness suffices.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
