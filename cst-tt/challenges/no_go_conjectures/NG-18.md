# NG-18 — Essential external support precludes target-native autonomy

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

If removal of an external support process necessarily destroys every realization of the declared package, the package is not autonomous on the declared target carrier.

## Smallest intended witness

A target whose temporal order, decoding, repair, or resource regulation is permanently supplied from outside the carrier boundary.

## Proposed proof spine

Apply the declared withdrawal intervention and show no target-only invariant region remains.

## Candidate impact

`TS-02;TS-03;TS-04;TS-05;TS-08;TS-09`

## Assumptions

declared target boundary; target-native autonomy definition

## Falsifier or escape condition

Redraw the carrier boundary before evaluation or prove endogenous regeneration after finite bootstrapping.

## Nonclaim

Does not settle consciousness of extended or distributed systems; it tests the declared boundary.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
