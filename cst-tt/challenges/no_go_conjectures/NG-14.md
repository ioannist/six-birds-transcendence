# NG-14 — Certificate match is independent of certificate soundness

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

Matching a structural certificate across carriers does not entail that the certificate tracks consciousness unless certificate soundness is separately assumed or established.

## Smallest intended witness

Two interpretations of the same formal certificate, one psychophysically sound and one not, share all structural facts.

## Proposed proof spine

The structural premises are invariant while the ontological interpretation varies.

## Candidate impact

`TS-02;TS-05;TS-06;TS-09`

## Assumptions

certificate soundness not derivable from structural syntax alone

## Falsifier or escape condition

A valid soundness theorem connecting the certificate to the intended ontological predicate.

## Nonclaim

Does not refute the certificate's structural usefulness.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
