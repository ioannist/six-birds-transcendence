# NG-16 — Symmetry–uniqueness–branch-success trilemma

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

In exact symmetric duplication, no criterion can simultaneously be symmetry-invariant, award success to each qualifying branch, and return exactly one unique continuer.

## Smallest intended witness

One source, two symmetric exact targets, both satisfying all branch-local gates.

## Proposed proof spine

Symmetry gives equal verdicts; branch success makes both positive; uniqueness forbids both.

## Candidate impact

`TS-01;TS-02;TS-03;TS-05;TS-06;TS-07;TS-08;TS-09;TS-10`

## Assumptions

exact symmetry; branch-local success

## Falsifier or escape condition

Reject one horn: add asymmetry, deny one branch, or make continuation relation-valued.

## Nonclaim

Does not privilege a particular horn.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
