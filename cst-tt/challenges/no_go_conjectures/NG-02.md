# NG-02 — Symmetric-duplication unique-selection impossibility

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

A source-relative rule invariant under target automorphisms cannot select exactly one of two perfectly symmetric qualifying targets.

## Smallest intended witness

One source and two isomorphic targets exchanged by an automorphism that fixes all admissible evidence.

## Proposed proof spine

Assume unique selection; apply the automorphism; invariance forces the selected target to map to itself, contradicting target exchange.

## Candidate impact

`TS-01;TS-02;TS-03;TS-05;TS-06;TS-08;TS-09;TS-10`

## Assumptions

symmetry and evidence invariance

## Falsifier or escape condition

An admissible asymmetry not fixed by the automorphism.

## Nonclaim

Does not decide whether both, neither, or a relation-valued pair should count as successors.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
