# NG-10 — Temporal-gap underdetermination without dormancy semantics

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

A gap containing no maintained target package is neither continuation nor failure by structural data alone unless a dormancy/recovery rule is added.

## Smallest intended witness

Two reconstructions after identical empty intervals, one source-derived and one independently generated, with equal endpoint state.

## Proposed proof spine

Remove history/dormancy data; the endpoint evidence is identical while intended ancestry differs.

## Candidate impact

`TS-02;TS-03;TS-04;TS-05;TS-07;TS-08;TS-09;TS-10`

## Assumptions

no canonical now or persistence through emptiness

## Falsifier or escape condition

A noncircular dormancy token and recovery law that uniquely carries the relevant object.

## Nonclaim

Does not prove gaps are fatal under every ontology.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
