# NG-01 — Public-shadow nonidentifiability

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

No criterion whose input is restricted to a public/report shadow can determine cross-carrier predictive transport or lineage over a class containing public-equivalent but predictively distinct systems.

## Smallest intended witness

Two finite machines share every admitted public trace but diverge on a hidden continuation probe after handoff.

## Proposed proof spine

Construct observationally equivalent source/target pairs; swap the hidden transition; invoke input invariance of the criterion.

## Candidate impact

`TS-01;TS-02;TS-03;TS-09;TS-10`

## Assumptions

finite admitted interface; hidden alternatives allowed

## Falsifier or escape condition

A proof that the declared public interface is continuation-complete for the model class.

## Nonclaim

Does not prove hidden structure is consciousness-relevant.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
