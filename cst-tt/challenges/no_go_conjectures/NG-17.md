# NG-17 — Fusion conflict for exclusive action-credit ledgers

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

Two source histories assigning exclusive credit or incompatible self-coordinates cannot be naively fused into one coherent ledger without a conflict-resolution rule that changes at least one input claim.

## Smallest intended witness

Sources credit incompatible agents for the same exclusive event; target copies both ledgers.

## Proposed proof spine

The conjunction violates exclusivity; any coherent target must revise, contextualize, or branch the claims.

## Candidate impact

`TS-02;TS-07;TS-08;TS-10`

## Assumptions

exclusive credit predicates retained

## Falsifier or escape condition

A typed provenance algebra that embeds both claims without contradiction.

## Nonclaim

Does not rule out fusion with transformed provenance.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
