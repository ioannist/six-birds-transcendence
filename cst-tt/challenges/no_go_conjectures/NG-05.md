# NG-05 — Permanent decoder support is not target autonomy

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

A target whose relevant package exists only while an external decoder/controller remains active fails a target-native autonomy predicate defined by withdrawal.

## Smallest intended witness

Encoded state with correct decoded behavior during support and immediate collapse when decoder is removed.

## Proposed proof spine

Evaluate the autonomy predicate under its declared intervention; the target-only trajectory exits the maintained set.

## Candidate impact

`TS-02;TS-04;TS-05;TS-09`

## Assumptions

autonomy defined by target-side maintenance after withdrawal

## Falsifier or escape condition

The decoder is included in the independently declared target carrier or is endogenously regenerated by it.

## Nonclaim

Does not say externally supported systems are unconscious.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
