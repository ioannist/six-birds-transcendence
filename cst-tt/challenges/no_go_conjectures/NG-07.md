# NG-07 — Small global residual can hide a catastrophic local defect

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

No scalar average error bound alone protects a hard local predicate over model classes permitting error concentration.

## Smallest intended witness

Move an epsilon-sized fraction of total state error onto the sole viability-critical transition.

## Proposed proof spine

Construct a sequence with aggregate residual tending to zero while the hard predicate remains false.

## Candidate impact

`TS-02;TS-04;TS-05;TS-09`

## Assumptions

localized hard gates; aggregate metric permits concentration

## Falsifier or escape condition

A stability/Lipschitz theorem tying the global metric to every critical local margin.

## Nonclaim

Does not rule out typed vector metrics or certified margins.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
