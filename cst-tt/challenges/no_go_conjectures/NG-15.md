# NG-15 — Structural lineage does not entail numerical personal identity

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

Any structural lineage relation that permits nontrivial fission or fusion cannot be identical to strict numerical identity, which is one-one and transitive in the ordinary sense.

## Smallest intended witness

One source relates to two symmetric successors, or two sources relate to one fusion target.

## Proposed proof spine

Compare the arity of the relation with identity's uniqueness laws.

## Candidate impact

`TS-03;TS-07;TS-08;TS-10`

## Assumptions

ordinary numerical identity laws

## Falsifier or escape condition

A revised identity concept explicitly relation-valued and no longer numerical identity.

## Nonclaim

Does not decide what matters for survival.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
