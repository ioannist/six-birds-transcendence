# NG-03 — Predictive-quotient insufficiency for the full package

**Status:** conjecture/theorem prospectus; **not proved in CST-EXP-04**.

## Setup and conjecture

Equality or bisimilarity of a selected predictive quotient does not imply preservation of independent binding, temporal-closure, action-credit, or viability predicates.

## Smallest intended witness

Two systems share quotient transitions but differ on a hidden joint-binding bit or repair dependency.

## Proposed proof spine

Take a product extension invisible to quotient tests but visible to a package predicate.

## Candidate impact

`TS-01;TS-09;TS-10`

## Assumptions

package predicates not definable from the quotient

## Falsifier or escape condition

A definability theorem deriving every retained package clause from the quotient.

## Nonclaim

Does not show predictive structure is unimportant.

The prospect does not establish target consciousness, personal identity, A2-P, or empirical recognizability. A later phase must state the exact finite model class and prove or refute the claim.
