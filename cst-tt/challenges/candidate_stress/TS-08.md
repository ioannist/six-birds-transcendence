# TS-08 — Reflexive Temporal–Credit Ledger Bridge: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `narrowed_to_mandatory_gate`
**Retained role:** temporal, reflexive, and action-credit provenance gate
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-01;SF-02`

## Result

Necessary for historically thick claims; insufficient as a full transported object.

- **Core strength:** tests whether self-coordinate, target-native time, and action-credit history are generated rather than copied as labels.
- **Decisive boundary:** ledger agreement can coexist with predictive, binding, viability, or recombination mismatch.
- **Required amendment:** Define conflict resolution under fusion, dormant intervals, and the relation between ledger preservation and live process causation.
- **Strongest salvage:** Use as a provenance/temporal gate where a candidate claims historical continuation.
