# TS-03 — Causal-Overlap Process Continuation: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `survives_as_family_core`
**Retained role:** conservative process-lineage core
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-02`

## Result

It survives because its restrictive verdicts are coherent and falsifiable, not because gradual replacement has been assumed successful.

- **Core strength:** distinguishes live overlapping handoff from detached copying through one causally connected moving-support process.
- **Decisive boundary:** the selected process organization can be circular or too coarse, and the criterion deliberately rejects no-overlap copies and unlicensed gaps.
- **Required amendment:** Specify the non-question-begging process invariant, target-native maintenance, temporal closure, and composition/confluence law.
- **Strongest salvage:** Retain as a stringent lineage family, not as a universal substrate-independence theorem.
