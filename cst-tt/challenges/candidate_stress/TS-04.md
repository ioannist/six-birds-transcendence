# TS-04 — Viability–Repair Control Transport: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `narrowed_to_mandatory_gate`
**Retained role:** autonomy and viability gate
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-01;SF-02`

## Result

Essential as a veto and autonomy test; insufficient as the transported object or lineage criterion.

- **Core strength:** tests endogenous maintenance, repair, resource closure, and post-withdrawal survival.
- **Decisive boundary:** viability can be shared by unrelated systems and does not fix predictive content, binding, or lineage.
- **Required amendment:** Bind viability to a declared package and critical local margins rather than a generic attractor or scalar score.
- **Strongest salvage:** Make the withdrawal/viability audit mandatory for constructive families.
