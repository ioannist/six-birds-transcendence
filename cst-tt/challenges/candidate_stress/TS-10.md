# TS-10 — Heterogeneous Predictive-Ancestry Relation: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `survives_as_family_core`
**Retained role:** relation-valued branching lineage core
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-03`

## Result

The most coherent branching semantics, provided it is not renamed strict numerical identity.

- **Core strength:** handles fission, fusion, empty targets, and symmetric duplication without inventing a unique continuer.
- **Decisive boundary:** ancestry can become too permissive or circular and does not guarantee integrated target realization or autonomy.
- **Required amendment:** Give a finite noncircular ancestry generator, composition laws, counterexamples, and separate target-package/autonomy gates.
- **Strongest salvage:** Retain as the principal relation-valued lineage family with predictive, route, and approximation modules.
