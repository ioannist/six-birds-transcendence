# TS-07 — Recombination–Holonomy Continuation Functor: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `narrowed_to_crosscutting_audit`
**Retained role:** route, branch, fusion, and recombination audit
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-01;SF-02;SF-03`

## Result

The strongest route-sensitive audit; its value is crosscutting rather than standalone.

- **Core strength:** represents path dependence, fission/fusion, recombination, and holonomy without forcing unique continuation.
- **Decisive boundary:** does not by itself establish a full target package, viability, or empirical recognition.
- **Required amendment:** Add typed approximation and autonomy interfaces and avoid treating a continuation functor as phenomenal identity.
- **Strongest salvage:** Require its route/recombination checks across every survivor family.
