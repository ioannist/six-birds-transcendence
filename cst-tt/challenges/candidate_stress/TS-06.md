# TS-06 — Closure–Gluing Fixed-Point Span: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `narrowed_to_mandatory_gate`
**Retained role:** global coherence and gluing gate
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-01`

## Result

Survives as a necessary structural audit, not as an independent transfer criterion.

- **Core strength:** blocks local-witness and branchwise-success fallacies by demanding compatible global realization.
- **Decisive boundary:** closure/gluing alone has no causal ancestry, autonomy, or personal-identity content and can depend on a chosen cover.
- **Required amendment:** Prove cover/representative robustness, define closure intrinsically, and connect gluing to target-native support.
- **Strongest salvage:** Use as the global package-coherence gate in constructive candidates.
