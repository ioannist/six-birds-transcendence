# TS-01 — Predictive-Quotient Bisimulation: adversarial disposition

**Phase disposition:** `narrowed_to_component`
**Retained role:** predictive-invariant component
**Survivor-family placement:** `SF-03`

## Result

Nontrivial and compositional at S-T, but not a standalone criterion for realization, autonomy, or same-subject continuation.

- **Core strength:** detects public/current counterfeits by comparing future-sufficient quotient dynamics.
- **Decisive boundary:** the quotient can agree while binding, action credit, viability, or full-package realization differs.
- **Required amendment:** Declare a continuation-complete test family, add recombination probes, and never infer full package or identity from quotient equivalence.
- **Strongest salvage:** Retain as the predictive-content module inside a broader ancestry or approximation family.
