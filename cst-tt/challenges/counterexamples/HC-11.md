# HC-11 — Fusion of two previously independent lineages

**Claim lanes:** `S-L;S-R`
**No-go links:** `NG-17`

## Construction

Merge two sources carrying incompatible histories, self-coordinates, or action-credit ledgers into one target.

## Adversarial point

Fusion requires a conflict and provenance law, not mere state aggregation.
