\begin{abstract}
We instantiate a certificate-based closure calculus (Six Birds Theory) on an exact-finite class of
kinetically constrained glass models, where kernels, stationary measures, witnesses, and zero
controls are exact ratios of integers and the remaining certificate evidence is deterministic
float64, labeled as such in each artifact. On the declared scope: the thermodynamic lens has a positive closure deficit exceeding its own
constrained-equilibrium baseline by a certified margin of $3/2$ (achieved $\approx 1.64$) --- and
that baseline is provably nonzero, since closure is a lumpability property, not an equilibrium
property (GT1). The Kovacs protocol yields an exact split-pair witness: two preparation histories
current-equal and future-different (GT2). All $512$ predicates definable from the declared energy
lens agree on a witness pair that a memory readout separates, constructively foreclosing the
static-order-parameter search on that declared class (GT3). A kill-test panel correctly executes
five constructed artifact regimes (GT4). Resolving the witness pair costs exactly one declared
memory scalar --- fictive temperature is a lawful memory coordinate, and the buyback ledger
demonstrates the mechanism without proving general single-scalar insufficiency (GT5). The
predictive-structure layer receives a shell-local strict-promotion certificate via
non-factorization, with the fuller completion-dynamics route explicitly not invoked (GT6).
Aging readouts frozen on the East model and transferred out-of-sample under a pre-registered
no-fitting protocol scored 8 passes and 14 one-sided failures, with the trap family structurally
unobservable for a reason we prove: the declared trap weight family's stationary distribution is
exactly temperature-invariant (GT7). A vendored Lean core machine-checks the abstract
witness/refinement theorems; attempting to encode the numeric witness produced a machine-checked
impossibility theorem locating that core's expressiveness boundary. Every claim is hash-pinned to
its artifacts, and the full nonclaims ledger is printed unabridged.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}


\subsection{The problem, and the question behind it}

Anderson called the nature of glass and the glass transition the deepest unsolved problem in
solid-state theory \cite{anderson-1995}. One face of that problem is sixty years old and
unusually concrete: no static order parameter for glass has ever been found. A glass and a liquid
can agree in density, energy, and every routinely monitored one-time observable, yet behave
differently; six decades of proposals for the missing static quantity have not produced one
\cite{berthier-biroli-2011}. A second face is equally concrete and sits in the laboratory record
since 1963: Kovacs's crossover experiment \cite{kovacs-1963}. Equilibrate a polymer glass hot,
quench it, age it, then jump the temperature up and watch its volume relax: at some moment the
volume \emph{equals} its new equilibrium value --- and does not stay there, overshooting through a
non-monotone hump before returning. At the crossing moment the system is indistinguishable from
equilibrium in the monitored observable and distinguishable from it in its future. The standard
partial fix, the Tool--Narayanaswamy--Moynihan fictive temperature
\cite{tool-1946,narayanaswamy-1971,moynihan-1976}, adds one memory scalar; the Kovacs literature's
verdict on single-parameter descriptions is that richer protocols defeat them.

This paper treats both faces as one formal question: \emph{does the declared panel of one-time
observables close?} That is --- does there exist an autonomous law in that vocabulary that loses
nothing --- and if not, what certifies the failure, what is the minimal state that repairs it, and
what does the repair cost?

\subsection{What this paper does}

We answer on an exact-finite audited class: kinetically constrained models
\cite{ritort-sollich-2003,garrahan-sollich-toninelli-2011} --- the East model
\cite{jackle-eisinger-1991} as substrate, Fredrickson--Andersen \cite{fredrickson-andersen-1984}
and a trap family \cite{bouchaud-1992,monthus-bouchaud-1996} as transfer targets --- at system
sizes where the kernels, witnesses, and controls are exact ratios of integers. The formal vocabulary (closure
deficits, packaging maps, predictive quotients, witnesses, promotion gates) is the Six Birds
Theory corpus \sbtcite{sbt-primer,sbt-foundations-i,sbt-foundations-ii,sbt-foundations-iii,sbt-foundations-iv,sbt-paper-a,sbt-paper-b,sbt-paper-c}; this paper instantiates it rather than
extending it, and every claim names its corpus anchor.

The results, each at its exact declared scope (\S\ref{sec:gt1}--\S\ref{sec:gt7}): the
thermodynamic lens has a positive closure deficit on the audited glassy scope, exceeding even its
own constrained-equilibrium baseline by a certified margin --- and, a correction to our own design
expectations, that baseline is provably nonzero, because closure is a lumpability property, not an
equilibrium property (GT1). The Kovacs protocol yields an exact split-pair witness:
current-equal, future-different (GT2). All $512$ predicates definable from the declared lens
agree on a witness pair that a memory readout separates --- the order-parameter search is
foreclosed \emph{on that declared class}, constructively (GT3). The memory candidate survives a
kill-test panel that correctly executes five constructed artifact regimes (GT4). The witness pair
costs exactly one declared scalar to resolve --- fictive temperature is a lawful memory coordinate,
and the shipped certificate demonstrates the buyback mechanism without proving general
single-scalar insufficiency (GT5). The predictive-structure layer receives a shell-local
strict-promotion certificate via non-factorization, with the stronger completion-dynamics route
explicitly not invoked (GT6). And the aging readouts were frozen, transferred out-of-sample under
a no-fitting protocol, and mechanically scored: 8 of 22 scored predictions passed, 14 failed
one-sidedly, and the trap family proved structurally out of the protocol's reach for a reason we
prove (GT7).

\subsection{How to read the claims}

Four contributions, in the order we would defend them. First, the \textbf{discipline}: an
end-to-end instantiation of a certificate methodology on a real physics target --- machine-readable
claim envelopes, declared finite quantifier domains, content-hash freezing, controls, and honest
negatives filed as first-class results, including the places where the discipline forced our own
claims to shrink. Second, the \textbf{result complex} itself: seven claims, four bridges, six
honest negatives, each hash-pinned to its artifacts (the bracketed labels throughout are
machine-checked citations into the claims ledger). Third, the \textbf{freeze-and-transfer
protocol with its actual scoreboard} --- the failures are results, with a clean one-sided pattern
and a proven structural explanation for the unobserved column. Fourth, the \textbf{Lean-checked
core plus a scope-boundary finding}: the attempt to encode the numeric witness in the vendored
formal core produced a machine-checked impossibility theorem instead, locating exactly which
certificates can live in that core and why.

What this paper is not: it proves nothing about all glasses, the continuum, or the ideal-glass
transition; it makes no irreversibility claims; and its field-facing shadow --- ``no static order
parameter'' --- is licensed only over the declared class it is proved on. The full nonclaims
ledger is printed, unabridged, in \S\ref{sec:discussion}.
 \section{Formal Architecture and Methods}
\label{sec:methods}


\subsection{The SBT objects this paper instantiates}

This paper is an instantiation, not a foundations paper: every formal object below is defined in
the Six Birds Theory (SBT) corpus \sbtcite{sbt-primer,sbt-foundations-i,sbt-foundations-ii,sbt-foundations-iii,sbt-foundations-iv,sbt-paper-a,sbt-paper-b,sbt-paper-c}, and we cite rather
than re-derive. Five objects carry all seven results.

\paragraph{The lens and its packaging map.} A \emph{finite theory package}
(F-I~\sbtcite{sbt-foundations-i}, Def.~1) fixes a finite state space $Z$ (here: micro
configurations of a kinetically constrained model, with the protocol phase lifted into the state),
a declared observable map $f$ --- the \emph{thermodynamic lens} $\Pi_{\mathrm{std}}$, in this build
the energy readout $L_{\mathrm{energy}}$ and its declared panel --- and a \emph{packaging endomap}
$E_{\tau,f}(\mu) = U_f(Q_f(\mu P^{\tau}))$: evolve $\tau$ steps under the model kernel, push to the
lens, re-lift via declared prototypes. The \emph{idempotence defect} $\delta_{\tau,f}$ (F-I
Def.~9) measures how far packaging is from a fixed point; aging is its persistent positivity.

\paragraph{Closure deficit.} For a lens $\Pi$ over a finite Markov law, the closure deficit
\[
  \mathrm{CD}_{\tau}(\Pi) \;=\; I\!\left(X_t;\, Y_{t+\tau} \,\middle|\, Y_t\right)
\]
(conditional mutual information between the micro state now and the lens value $\tau$ steps ahead,
given the lens value now; F-III~\sbtcite{sbt-foundations-iii} T8; \sbtcite{sbt-paper-b} Def.~3.1)
is zero iff the lens is $\tau$-lumpable: iff an autonomous law in lens vocabulary loses nothing.
$\mathrm{CD}_{\tau}>0$ is therefore an exact lower bound on what \emph{any} autonomous
thermodynamic-vocabulary law of the system must discard. Two structural facts matter for reading
our GT1 result: $\mathrm{CD}_{\tau}=0$ is a lumpability property of the (kernel, lens) pair, not
an equilibrium property; and it is protocol-relative in the aging regime, where the law is
time-inhomogeneous (the protocol-relative extension is this project's GB2 demo).

\paragraph{Quotients and witnesses.} Over a declared finite catalog of preparation histories and
continuation protocols, the \emph{current quotient} $Q$ identifies histories with equal current
lens signatures, and the \emph{predictive quotient} $M$ identifies histories with equal future
signatures over the declared catalog (\sbtcite{sbt-paper-a}, Defs.~2.2--2.3). The comparison map
$\pi: M \to Q$ always exists; a \emph{predictive witness} (split pair) is a pair of histories with
$h \equiv^{0} h'$ but $h \not\equiv^{+} h'$ --- current-equal, future-different
(\sbtcite{sbt-paper-a}, Def.~2.4). Witnesses exist iff $\pi$ is strictly refining
(\sbtcite{sbt-paper-a}, Thm.~3.5; machine-checked as
\texttt{strictRefinement\_iff\_nonempty\_predictiveWitness}, \S\ref{sec:lean}).

\paragraph{Claim grades and nonclaims.} Every result carries exactly one F-II grade
(\sbtcite{sbt-foundations-ii}, \S9.3): \emph{theorem-grade (audited class)} --- proved or audited
unconditionally within the covered finite scope, at the artifact's declared evidence type (exact
rational or deterministic float64); \emph{certificate-grade (shell-local)} --- the [C]
standard \sbtcite{sbt-paper-c}, valid on the frozen shell with no broader-class transfer;
\emph{run-only readout}; \emph{recognition-grade}; or \emph{control}. Every artifact carries a
machine-readable envelope (\texttt{claim\_id}, \texttt{claim\_grade}, \texttt{quantifier\_domain},
\texttt{nonclaims}) validated against a schema in the reproducibility package. Nonclaims are not
boilerplate: the full nonclaims ledger is printed in \S\ref{sec:discussion}, and several are
load-bearing (e.g.\ GT6's verdict basis, \S\ref{sec:gt6}).

\subsection{Exact arithmetic and the freeze discipline}

Exact rational arithmetic (Python \texttt{fractions.Fraction}) carries the kernels, stationary
measures, witness gaps, the GT3 predicate sweep, the trap-invariance lemma, the GT1
idempotence-defect ratio ($289/121$), and the lumpable zero control (whose CD is exactly $0$
because exact lumpability is verified symbolically). Not everything theorem-graded is rational,
and each artifact says which it is: GT1's CD grid is deterministic float64 evidence
(\texttt{evidence\_type: float64\_deterministic} in the artifact itself), and its declared margin
$3/2$ is checked in float64 --- the claim's grade rests on the audited-class quantification plus
the artifact's declared evidence type, not on a pretended exactness. Float64 also appears in
explicitly labeled diagnostics (spectral gaps, windowed CD diagnostics, GT7 Monte Carlo spot
checks, the GT6 pressure closure). Controls accompany every certificate, including the
constructed negative controls of the triage panel (\S\ref{sec:gt3-gt4}).

Pre-registration (GT7, \S\ref{sec:gt7}) and the paper's own evidence base are frozen by sha256
content hashes over canonical JSON --- each freeze artifact carries a \texttt{content\_hash} and a
\texttt{freeze\_note}, and every cross-artifact citation pins the target's hash. Regenerating any
artifact invalidates, detectably, every citation of it; a repository-wide test suite enforces all
such bindings, and the paper's own claim citations (the bracketed row labels throughout, e.g.\
[GT1]) are pinned the same way: each carries a hidden artifact hash checked against the claims
ledger and the current artifact bytes at build time. The project deliberately does not use
version-control history as its freeze mechanism; the content hashes are the proof object.

 \section{Models and Pipeline}
\label{sec:models}


\subsection{Model families}

Four model families populate the audited class; all are finite-state discrete-time Markov chains
with exactly rational kernel entries. Temperature never enters as a free real parameter: we
declare a finite rational grid in the Boltzmann activation variable
$\epsilon \in \{1/10, 1/5, 3/10, 2/5, 1/2, 3/5, 7/10\}$, where $\epsilon$ plays the role of
$e^{-\beta}$ and the nominal temperature $T = -1/\ln\epsilon$ is a bridge annotation only. The
up-spin equilibrium concentration $c = \epsilon/(1+\epsilon)$ is exactly rational. Protocol
schedules are finite words over this grid, and the schedule phase is lifted into the state so the
chain stays autonomous (the clock-audit discipline of F-I \sbtcite{sbt-foundations-i}).

\paragraph{East model (primary substrate).} $N$ binary spins with a frozen up-spin wall at site
$0$; spin $i$ may flip only if its left neighbor is up ($C_i(n) = n_{i-1}$)
\cite{jackle-eisinger-1991}. One step of the random-scan heat-bath kernel $K_\epsilon$: pick a
site uniformly; if unconstrained, resample it from the Bernoulli($c$) marginal. Because the
constraint does not involve the flipping spin itself, $K_\epsilon$ is reversible w.r.t.\ the
product measure $\pi_\epsilon(n) \propto \epsilon^{E(n)}$ with $E(n) = \sum_i n_i$ --- verified
programmatically as exact Fraction identities for every $\epsilon$ on the grid. Note for what
follows: $K_\epsilon$ and $K_{\epsilon'}$ are reversible w.r.t.\ \emph{different} measures, which
is exactly the hypothesis failure of the published protocol-trap theorem that the GB1 demo
(\S\ref{sec:gt3-gt4}) addresses.

\paragraph{Fredrickson--Andersen (FA-1f).} Identical structure with the symmetric constraint
$C_i(n) = \max(n_{i-1}, n_{i+1})$ \cite{fredrickson-andersen-1984}. FA is the transfer target for
GT7: nothing about FA is used in fitting or freezing; it is run only after predictions are
committed (\S\ref{sec:gt7}).

\paragraph{Unconstrained control.} The same heat-bath chain with $C_i \equiv 1$. This model is
structurally lumpable w.r.t.\ the energy lens, so its closure deficit is exactly $0$ as a rational
number --- the zero control for GT1 (\S\ref{sec:gt1}).

\paragraph{Bouchaud-type trap model (second transfer target).} $M$ traps with declared rational
escape weights $w_k$; from trap $k$, jump to a uniformly random trap with probability proportional
to $w_k$, else stay \cite{bouchaud-1992,monthus-bouchaud-1996}. Temperature dependence is a
declared finite family of rational weight vectors per grid $\epsilon$ (exponentiating weights
would break rationality); the physical Arrhenius relation is an \texttt{Ann\_Bridge} annotation.
The stationary measure is $\pi_k \propto 1/w_k$, verified exactly.

\subsection{A proven limitation of the declared trap family}

\claimref{FINDING.trap\_epsilon\_invariance}{671a28b4a4a9d812a1a6cb6e0083fc23ac01360c6ef5f6327b99de6560c153c2}
The declared trap weight family multiplies every trap's base weight by a common per-$\epsilon$
scalar. Since $\pi_k \propto 1/w_k$ depends only on weight \emph{ratios}, that scalar cancels
exactly: the trap stationary distribution is $\epsilon$-invariant (proved in exact Fraction
arithmetic; regression-tested). The declared temperature action therefore governs relaxation
\emph{rate} only, never the relaxation \emph{target}. This is known in substance in the trap-model
literature
\cite{bouchaud-1992,monthus-bouchaud-1996,diezemann-heuer-2011,bertin-bouchaud-drouffe-godreche-2003}:
the standard convention is a per-trap Arrhenius factor $w_k(T) \propto e^{-E_k/T}$, under which
different traps rescale by different temperature-dependent factors and the equilibrium genuinely
shifts --- exactly the mechanism used to produce Kovacs memory in trap models
\cite{diezemann-heuer-2011,bertin-bouchaud-drouffe-godreche-2003}. Our multiplicative-scalar
choice was made deliberately to keep every computation exactly rational; the invariance is its
elementary, proven consequence. It is filed here as a design caveat because it has a concrete
downstream cost, paid honestly in \S\ref{sec:gt7}: a Kovacs protocol cannot start the trap family
below its post-jump equilibrium, so the trap column of the transfer scoreboard is structurally
unobservable.

\nonclaim{Not a novelty claim: the cancellation is standard trap-model structure once stated; the
  contribution is stating, proving, and paying for it within the declared family. It says nothing
  about trap dynamics or aging trajectories; only the stationary distribution is
  $\epsilon$-invariant.}

\subsection{The control structure, including a corrected expectation}

The controls deserve emphasis because one of them corrected our own design document. The intended
design called for constrained-East equilibrium as a zero-CD control. That expectation is
\emph{false}, and provably so: $\mathrm{CD}_\tau = 0$ iff the (kernel, lens) pair is

constraint depends on \emph{where} excitations sit, not merely how many there are, so East is
non-lumpable w.r.t.\ $L_{\mathrm{energy}}$ even at stationarity. The shipped certificate
therefore uses two controls with different jobs (Fig.~\ref{fig:gt1-ladder}): the structurally
lumpable \emph{unconstrained} model sits at exact rational $\mathrm{CD} = 0$ (float residuals at
$10^{-17}$, exact lumpability verified symbolically), while constrained-East equilibrium is an
honest \emph{nonzero baseline} --- the certificate's content is that aging exceeds even that
baseline by the declared margin. Equilibrium is not used as a zero control anywhere.

 \section{GT2 and GT5: The Kovacs Witness and the Buyback Curve}
\label{sec:gt2-gt5}


\subsection{The Kovacs protocol on the audited class}

The Kovacs protocol \cite{kovacs-1963} --- equilibrate hot, quench cold, age for $t_w$, up-jump to
an intermediate temperature, watch a one-time observable relax --- was located on the East
substrate by an exact Phase-0 search (a pre-certificate control, filed as such): at $N=10$,
$\epsilon: 7/10 \to 1/10 \to 2/5$ with $t_w = 40$, the energy trace crosses its target-equilibrium
value at $t_K = 1$ and overshoots through a hump peaking at $t_{\mathrm{peak}} = 35$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:kovacs-hump}), all in exact rational arithmetic. At $t_K$ the system equals
equilibrium in the monitored observable; its subsequent non-monotone excursion is the classic
signature that the current value does not determine the future.
