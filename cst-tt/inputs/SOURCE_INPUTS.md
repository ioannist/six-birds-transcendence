## Handling rulings

- The source archives are inputs, not empirical evidence.
- The owner-excluded paper named in the normative plan remains quarantined and must not be used as a source.
- Papers 42 and 60 are versions of one Strict Audited Utility research line; they are not independent evidence. Later statement extraction should use the newer revision as normative unless a version difference matters.
- Existing results must be marked fixed-support where applicable.
- The project may introduce new definitions only when they are marked `new` rather than attributed to the corpus.
- No source file may be silently skipped during CST-EXP-01; non-primary cards are still required.
