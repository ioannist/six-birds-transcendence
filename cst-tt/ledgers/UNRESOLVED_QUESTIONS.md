# Unresolved Questions After CST-EXP-00

These questions are deliberately unresolved. They define work for later phases rather than defects in the charter.

## Carrier and support

1. What is the strongest operational criterion for “genuinely distinct carrier” when source and target share hardware during overlap?
2. Should a medium be modeled as a carrier class, an implementation category, a resource law, or several alternative notions?
3. Can a distributed package straddle source and target carriers without creating a third joint carrier, and what typing is best?
4. Which fixed-support results can be generalized by a span/common-refinement construction, and which require new mathematics?

## Transported object

5. Is the best candidate a terminal predictive quotient, a transition algebra, a closure fixed point, a viability-maintenance law, a full C-package, or a layered tuple?
6. Is state-level transport ever sufficient, or must every serious candidate be history/process-level?
7. Which C-package components are invariant-like, and which are necessarily rebuilt or re-realized on the target?
8. Can hiddenness be preserved when the source and target interfaces differ radically?

## Bridge and route

9. Is a function too restrictive, and is a relation too weak, for the core bridge?
10. Should gradual replacement be represented as an overlap process, a sequence of spans, or a changing-support carrier?
11. What composition law can accommodate exact and defective bridges?
12. What flatness/confluence condition would be strong enough for route independence without trivializing route-sensitive memory?
13. Can a route legitimately pass through a period in which no full package is instantiated?

## Lineage

14. Should the project seek a positive lineage criterion or primarily a non-uniqueness/no-go theorem?
15. How should symmetric duplication be represented: two successors, no unique successor, indeterminate token identity, or different outputs for different criteria?
16. What source-specific structure can survive fusion without contradiction?
17. Can a dormant interval preserve lineage through a record object, or does that merely enable reconstruction?
18. Is relation-valued lineage enough for the intended project, or is a token-level criterion necessary for the final interpretation?

## Autonomy and maintenance

19. Which intervention best distinguishes target realization from a persistent readout of the source?
20. Is finite-horizon autonomy scientifically useful, or must the target support an invariant maintenance region?
21. Can an external environment remain part of target autonomy while an external decoder cannot, and how is that boundary drawn?
22. How should endogenous error correction be distinguished from continuous reconstruction by an external protocol?

## Approximation and recognition

23. Which defect family is appropriate: factorization, simulation, closure, predictive divergence, viability margin, recombination, or a vector of defects?
24. Can small structural defects be shown to preserve lineage classification away from bifurcation/viability boundaries?
25. Which hidden structures are empirically identifiable even in principle?
26. What would count as a public-perfect counterfeit once the target has interactive, adaptive behavior?

## Assumptions and target strategy

27. Can a useful structural cross-carrier theorem be obtained without any use of A2?
28. Is there a narrower, formal replacement for A2 that concerns only invariance of a named package rather than ontology?
29. Can certificate soundness be decomposed clause-by-clause rather than purchased globally?
30. Which negative result would remain valuable if every constructive lineage target fails?
