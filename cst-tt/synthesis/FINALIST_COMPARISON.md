
# CST-EXP-06 Finalist Comparison and Target Selection

**Status:** final exploration comparison; target freeze complete.
**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Primary:** `FT-01` — Finite Integrated Autonomous Causal Handoff.
**Fallback:** `FT-02` — Integrated Autonomous Predictive Successorship.
**Mandatory companion / terminal fallback:** `FT-NG` — shared finite no-go kernel.

## 1. Comparison rule

The three CST-EXP-05 survivor families answer different lanes. A complete constructive target for this project must keep those lanes separate while jointly representing:

1. **S-R:** an integrated target-native package rather than a public shadow;
2. **S-L:** an explicitly declared source-target structural relation;
3. **A:** post-withdrawal target autonomy.

Consequently, the standalone landing bundles remain indispensable modules but are not by themselves final constructive targets. The paired architectures are the true finalists. The no-go kernel is evaluated as a mandatory companion and terminal fallback, not as a positive transfer theory.

The scorecard uses the governing-plan hard gates plus one explicit project-completeness gate, `HG-10`. Soft scores are transparent decision aids on a 0–5 scale with weights totaling 100. They are not evidence, and a small numerical difference is not a scientific result.

## 3. Why FT-01 is primary

`FT-01` is the narrowest architecture that jointly addresses target realization, lineage, and autonomy while making **interior process history** do real mathematical work. It distinguishes a live staged handoff from an exact detached copy even when the copy has the same endpoint state, the same finite package certificate, and target autonomy. The distinguishing condition is not intuition or substrate labels: it is a separately audited no-gap mixed-support corridor containing at least one causally nonredundant source-target update, followed by target takeover and successful withdrawal.

This is the best first meaning of *move* available in the explored formal space. It is conservative: destructive reconstruction does not satisfy the primary lineage relation. That exclusion is a criterion boundary, not a theorem that consciousness cannot survive destructive uploading.

## 4. Why FT-02 is the fallback

`FT-02` addresses the strongest exact case excluded by the primary. It permits a source-derived destructive reconstruction to be a relation-valued structural successor when a finite operational provenance seed, predictive bridge, no-ex-nihilo closure, target package, and autonomy are all established. It excludes independently constructed twins and refuses unique identity under symmetric duplication.

The fallback is activated only if the live-corridor criterion is shown to be circular, noncompositional, or too restrictive for the intended scientific target. It is not an automatic broadening after a failed proof.

## 5. Why the dual-lineage architecture is deferred

`FT-03` would retain both `LiveHandoff` and `PredictiveAncestry`, ideally proving an inclusion such as `LiveHandoff ⊆ PredictiveAncestry` and an exact destructive-reconstruction separation. That is scientifically attractive but too broad for the first theorem-and-toy project. It requires both lineage engines and a nonarbitrary theorem connecting them. It becomes a later unification target after one engine has landed.

## 6. Why the no-go kernel is mandatory

Every constructive attempt must ship with exact boundaries:

- public/report equality does not identify hidden package or process structure;
- symmetric targets admit no invariant unique selector;
- branch marginals need not preserve recombination;
- a permanently external decoder does not establish target autonomy;
- endpoint equality does not establish live-process lineage;
- local certificates need not glue globally;
- path-equivalent public endpoints may retain route holonomy;
- a small scalar residual can hide a local hard-gate failure.

If both constructive targets fail their circularity or nonvacuity gates, these results become the terminal landing rather than discarded preparatory work.

## 7. Full finalist profiles


### F-01 — Integrated target package plus withdrawal autonomy

- **Architecture:** LB-01 / SF-01
- **Status:** `not_selected_component_only`
- **Strongest case:** It is the only standalone family that directly audits an integrated target-native package and withdrawal autonomy.
- **Strongest counterexample:** An autonomous package-equivalent independent twin passes S-R and A while lacking source lineage.
- **Unique insight:** Target realization and target-native maintenance are independent of ancestry.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** S13/S28 external-decoder and delayed-collapse cases.
- **Assumptions spent:** H-SOURCE-FULL; H-PRESERVE-O; H-GLOBAL-GLUE; H-TARGET-FULL; H-AUTONOMY
- **Theorem/toy burden:** TH-04; TH-05; TH-06 plus package/gluing/autonomy toys.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** No noncircular finite package witness or no target-owned post-withdrawal invariant region.

### F-02 — Finite causal-overlap process lineage

- **Architecture:** LB-02 / SF-02
- **Status:** `not_selected_component_only`
- **Strongest case:** It gives the cleanest process-sensitive reason to distinguish gradual handoff from detached copying.
- **Strongest counterexample:** A source-derived destructive reconstruction may preserve every declared endpoint structure while failing the live-overlap criterion.
- **Unique insight:** A continuing causal process is route/history data, not an endpoint property.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** S01/S07 gradual or prolonged overlap versus S02/S06 detached reconstruction/gap.
- **Assumptions spent:** H-LINEAGE; H-BRIDGE-ADM; H-XCI for the selected process signature; H-AUTONOMY if paired with takeover.
- **Theorem/toy burden:** TH-07; TH-08; TH-22 and an event-graph corridor checker.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** Every nonredundancy/process definition is verdict-loaded, or valid corridors do not compose.

### F-03 — Least relation-valued predictive ancestry

- **Architecture:** LB-03 / SF-03
- **Status:** `not_selected_component_only`
- **Strongest case:** It handles destructive reconstruction, fission, fusion, empty targets, and independent twins without arbitrary uniqueness.
- **Strongest counterexample:** A permissive provenance seed can act as an ancestry oracle and make the least closure formally correct but scientifically empty.
- **Unique insight:** Cross-carrier succession may be a least relation rather than a function or equivalence relation.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** S02/S03/S05/S09/S10 destructive reconstruction, copying, fission, and fusion.
- **Assumptions spent:** Operational Seed; monotone Γ; no-ex-nihilo; H-LINEAGE; object-specific H-XCI.
- **Theorem/toy burden:** TH-01; TH-09; TH-10; TH-11 plus quotient and closure tools.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** No operational seed excludes independent twins without already encoding successorhood.

### FT-01 — Finite Integrated Autonomous Causal Handoff (FIACH)

- **Architecture:** SF-01 + SF-02; mandatory route/recombination/approximation controls
- **Status:** `PRIMARY_FROZEN`
- **Strongest case:** It is the narrowest target that jointly establishes target package, source-derived live process lineage, and target autonomy while rejecting detached exact copies for a substantive interior-history reason.
- **Strongest counterexample:** A destructive source-record-target reconstruction can be exact, autonomous, and source-derived yet fails the required live mixed-support corridor.
- **Unique insight:** A defensible structural precursor to 'moving' requires a target-native package plus a continuing process and a successful withdrawal, not endpoint equality.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** Gradual replacement and live migration with a mixed nonredundant stage, contrasted with detached copying and decoder shadows.
- **Assumptions spent:** D-CARRIER; H-DISTINCT; H-BRIDGE-ADM; H-SOURCE-FULL (toy stipulation); H-PRESERVE-O; H-GLOBAL-GLUE; H-LINEAGE; H-AUTONOMY; route conditions when claimed.
- **Theorem/toy burden:** TH-04/05/06/07/08 plus combined certificate and composition lemmas; TP-03/04/07/08/10.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** Corridor circularity, noncomposition, absence of a nontrivial integrated target witness, or inability to internalize all essential support before withdrawal.

### FT-02 — Integrated Autonomous Predictive Successorship (IAPS)

- **Architecture:** SF-01 + SF-03; mandatory twin/symmetry/route controls
- **Status:** `FALLBACK_FROZEN`
- **Strongest case:** It retains target realization and autonomy while allowing source-derived destructive reconstruction and relation-valued branching.
- **Strongest counterexample:** Operational provenance may be too cheap: a record path can exist without carrying the organization that should ground succession.
- **Unique insight:** The strongest defensible result for copying may be autonomous source-derived successorship, not unique continuation.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** Destructive reconstruction, delayed activation with declared dormancy/provenance, fission, and fusion.
- **Assumptions spent:** All SF-01 structural assumptions plus operational Seed, monotone Γ, no-ex-nihilo, predictive bridge admissibility, and H-LINEAGE.
- **Theorem/toy burden:** TH-01/04/05/06/09/10/11 plus integrated successor theorem; TP-01/03/06/07/08/10.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** The ancestry seed is circular/universal, independent twins enter, or target package/autonomy cannot be independently established.

### FT-03 — Layered dual-lineage autonomous handoff

- **Architecture:** SF-01 + SF-02 + SF-03 with Live ⊆ Anc as an additional theorem target
- **Status:** `DEFERRED_UNIFICATION`
- **Strongest case:** It represents the live-handoff and destructive-reconstruction notions simultaneously and could prove their exact inclusion/separation.
- **Strongest counterexample:** Two independently contestable lineage criteria plus the full package may make the first project broad enough that no decisive theorem lands cleanly.
- **Unique insight:** There may be a hierarchy of structural continuation grades rather than one binary predicate.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** The complete scenario atlas, especially the live/destructive split.
- **Assumptions spent:** Union of FT-01 and FT-02 plus a theorem connecting overlap corridors to ancestry seeds.
- **Theorem/toy burden:** Almost the whole constructive reconnaissance bank and both lineage engines.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** The unification theorem depends on arbitrary seed choices or prevents a bounded first landing.

### FT-NG — Shared finite no-go and underdetermination kernel

- **Architecture:** LB-NG
- **Status:** `MANDATORY_COMPANION_TERMINAL_FALLBACK`
- **Strongest case:** Its symmetry, public-shadow, gluing, recombination, decoder, endpoint, route, and residual countermodels remain useful under every constructive choice.
- **Strongest counterexample:** Each no-go can become trivial if its information boundary or criterion class is drawn too narrowly.
- **Unique insight:** Many attractive transfer tests are provably insufficient even when no positive criterion is yet accepted.
- **Scenario where it outperforms alternatives:** Symmetric duplication, public-perfect counterfeits, decoder dependence, local/global obstruction, and route holonomy.
- **Assumptions spent:** Finite typed structures and explicitly restricted observation/criterion classes only.
- **Theorem/toy burden:** TH-13/14/16/17/18/19/20/22/23 and tiny finite witnesses.
- **Implementation abandonment trigger:** The statement omits its information boundary or is defeated by an obvious complete-interface exception.


## 8. Freeze decision

The exploration freezes:

- **Primary:** `FT-01 / FIACH`, a relation-valued finite integrated autonomous causal handoff.
- **Fallback:** `FT-02 / IAPS`, a relation-valued integrated autonomous predictive successor.
- **Companion:** `FT-NG`, the exact finite insufficiency and underdetermination suite.

No theorem has been proved and no toy program has been written. The selection specifies what the later project should attempt, what controls it must pass, and what conclusions remain excluded.
