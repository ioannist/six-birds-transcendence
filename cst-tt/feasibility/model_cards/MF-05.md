# MF-05 — Public-perfect hidden/package counterfeit

**Status:** exact finite reconnaissance model; specified but not implemented.
**Governing-plan slot:** hidden-counterfeit model
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## Purpose

Show that report equality and even a shallow certificate can coexist with a predictive, binding, or target-ownership failure.

## Exact finite signature

- **Carriers:** Positive target X_T+ and counterfeit X_T- are distinct eight-state systems with the same public interface alphabet.
- **States:** State=(r,h,b), r,h,b∈{0,1}; r is report, h is hidden predictive memory, b is a binding parity bit.  A certificate surface reports only r and a declared checksum c=r.
- **Inputs/interventions:** report,probe,bind.  The admitted public channel exposes report only; internal audit probes can use probe and bind.
- **Transition law:** Positive target updates r←h on probe and b←r xor h on bind.  Counterfeit preserves every report command but fixes h=0 after handoff or sets b=0 independently.
- **Interfaces/probes:** Public/report traces and shallow certificate c agree at the handoff and under report commands; predictive and joint-binding probes distinguish the systems.
- **Bridge/episode data:** Both receive the same public initialization.  Only the positive bridge carries and target-internalizes h and the binding law.

## Positive witness

Internal probe suite recovers the expected predictive partition and binding parity on X_T+.

## Exact negative or counterfeit control

X_T- passes public/report equality and the shallow certificate but fails at least one hidden package obligation.

## Required witness package

Public trace equality; one hidden distinguishing word; one joint-binding mismatch; certificate/pass-versus-package/fail table.

## Controls

Expose h publicly; remove binding from the package; use a complete finite interface and demonstrate that the no-go premise then fails.
