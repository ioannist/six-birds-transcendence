# MF-06 — Fission, fusion, and recombination parity

**Status:** exact finite reconnaissance model; specified but not implemented.
**Governing-plan slot:** fission/fusion and recombination model
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## Purpose

Separate branchwise preservation from joint recombination and expose fusion conflicts in exclusive action-credit histories.

## Exact finite signature

- **Carriers:** A four-state joint source X_S, two two-state branch carriers X_A,X_B, and a four-state fused target X_F.
- **States:** Source state=(x,y)∈{0,1}² with joint invariant p=x xor y.  Branch A carries x and credit label α; branch B carries y and exclusive credit label β.
- **Inputs/interventions:** split,recombine,credit_query.
- **Transition law:** split projects to x and y.  Positive recombination returns (x,y,p=x xor y).  Counterfeit recombination returns the same marginals but p=0.  A fused exclusive-credit field cannot simultaneously equal α and β when α≠β.
- **Interfaces/probes:** Branch interfaces expose only their marginal bit and local credit label; a recombination probe exposes p and a fusion audit requests one coherent credit law.
- **Bridge/episode data:** Projection branches plus a typed recombination map; fusion is admissible only with a declared conflict-resolution function for credit.

## Positive witness

Both branches preserve marginals and the positive fused target preserves parity with a nonexclusive provenance set {α,β}.

## Exact negative or counterfeit control

Counterfeit has identical branch data but wrong parity, or claims one exclusive credit owner while both histories remain active.

## Required witness package

Marginal equality table; parity counterexample; branch relation; fusion provenance/conflict record.

## Controls

Factorizable joint invariant; equal credit labels; branch deletion before fusion; no recombination probe.

- **What it separates:** SF-03 relation-valued fission/fusion from pairwise matching; SF-01 global binding/gluing; SF-02 genuine process fork/merge from endpoint aggregation.
- **Family roles:** SF-01=global package control;SF-02=process fork/fusion control;SF-03=core relation-valued positive

## Minimality rationale

Two binary branch variables are the smallest classical construction with identical marginals and a nontrivial joint parity witness.

## Formalization path

Finite products, projections, XOR, relations, and a partial fusion operation guarded by a conflict predicate.

## Kill condition

All admitted joint probes factor through marginals, or fusion credit is defined so weakly that incompatible exclusive claims never conflict.

## Explicit nonclaim

Recombination preservation and provenance consistency do not establish consciousness or personal identity after fission/fusion.
