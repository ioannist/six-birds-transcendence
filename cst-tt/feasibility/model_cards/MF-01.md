**Status:** exact finite reconnaissance model; specified but not implemented.
**Governing-plan slot:** cross-carrier predictive automata

## Purpose

Separate current/public matching from future-sufficient predictive structure on two disjoint carrier tokens.

## Exact finite signature

- **Carriers:** Distinct tokens X_S and X_T, each with four states; carrier identity is declared before any bridge verdict.
- **States:** X_S={s_(o,h): o,h∈{0,1}} and X_T={t_(o,h): o,h∈{0,1}}.  The current lens returns o; the hidden memory coordinate h is not in the current record.
- **Inputs/interventions:** U={tick,flip}; public trials may use tick at the handoff state, while the predictive probe family includes words of length at most two over both inputs.
- **Transition law:** tick sends (o,h)↦(h,h); flip sends (o,h)↦(o,1-h).  The positive target has the same typed law under φ(s_(o,h))=t_(o,h).  The counterfeit target replaces tick by (o,h)↦(0,h).
- **Interfaces/probes:** Current lens L(o,h)=o; predictive trace probe Π_H records L along every input word of length ≤2.  Thus s_(0,0) and s_(0,1) are current-equal but predictive-distinct.
- **Bridge/episode data:** Positive bridge R_+=graph(φ), with identity input map and stage-local clock map.  Counterfeit bridge R_- collapses h before target initialization.

## Positive witness

R_+ is saturated for the predictive equivalence and induces a quotient bisimulation; exact target carrier and source carrier remain distinct tokens.

## Exact negative or counterfeit control

R_- preserves the handoff public record o=0 but fails the length-one tick probe for h=1.  A public-only criterion returns a false positive.

## Required witness package

Predictive partition; saturated relation; one distinguishing input word; quotient transition table; exact failure pair.

## Controls
