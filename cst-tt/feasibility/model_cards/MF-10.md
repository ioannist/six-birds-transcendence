## Purpose

Give exact positive and negative autonomy cases near a maintained closure boundary.

## Exact finite signature

- **Carriers:** One source and two target variants with identical nominal output laws but different target-owned repair resources.
- **States:** Health state H={safe,warning,failed}; budget b∈{0,1}; viable set K={(safe,b),(warning,1)}.
- **Inputs/interventions:** tick,hit,repair,withdraw.
- **Transition law:** hit sends safe→warning and warning→failed.  A target-owned repair with b=1 sends warning→safe and consumes then regenerates b on the next safe tick.  The boundary target lacks regeneration after withdrawal.
- **Interfaces/probes:** Public output is identical in safe and warning.  Autonomy audit observes target-owned transition/resource graph and all allowed disturbance sequences with at most one hit per two ticks.
- **Bridge/episode data:** Handoff ends in warning with b=1.  Positive target remains in K under the declared disturbance class; negative target reaches failed after withdrawal because repair or budget regeneration was external.
