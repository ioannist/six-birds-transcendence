## Purpose

Check saturated typed relations between distinct finite carriers and produce a failing transition witness.

## Input types

Two finite transition systems; state relation R; input/clock maps; public or predictive observation maps; reachability scopes.

## Exact outputs

simulation/bisimulation verdict; saturation verdict; unmatched source or target step; largest simulation relation when requested.

## Witness and certificate format
