# TP-06 — Recombination-factorization tester

**Status:** later toy-program contract; no code written in CST-EXP-05.
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## Purpose

Determine whether joint probes factor through branch marginals and produce parity/correlation counterexamples.

## Input types

Finite joint source states; branch projection maps; target branch states; recombination map; finite joint probe family.

## Exact outputs

Branchwise preservation table; factorization verdict for each joint probe; recombination preservation verdict; minimal distinguishing joint state pair.

## Witness and certificate format

Two source/joint assignments with equal marginals and unequal probe value; projection and recombination tables.

## Positive and negative controls

MF-06 XOR parity; a factorable sum probe; missing-branch data; equal versus conflicting credit labels.

## Expected complexity

O(|X_joint|²·|Π_joint|) by exhaustive finite comparison; exponential in branch count when joint state is represented as a product.

## Candidate/theorem falsification role

A claim that branchwise or pairwise matching automatically preserves binding, recombination, or fusion provenance.

- **Models:** MF-05;MF-06
- **Theorem prospectuses:** TH-16;TH-24

## Lean/Python division

- **Lean kernel:** Formalize factorization and the finite parity counterexample.
- **Python reconnaissance/implementation:** Search joint tables and emit minimal counterexample pairs.

## Explicit nonclaim

A passed probe family may still omit other joint structure.
