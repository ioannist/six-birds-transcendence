# TP-10 — Finite counterexample and minimal-witness enumerator

**Status:** later toy-program contract; no code written in CST-EXP-05.
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## Purpose

Systematically search bounded finite carriers for the smallest positive witness or failure of a proposed implication.

## Input types

Bounded state counts; typed carrier/bridge schema; candidate predicates; symmetry constraints; theorem antecedent and negated consequent.

## Exact outputs

Smallest model by declared size order; complete transition/bridge tables; isomorphism-reduced witness class; UNSAT/exhaustion record within bounds.

## Witness and certificate format

Canonical finite structure, theorem-field truth table, automorphism group summary, and reproducible enumeration parameters.

## Positive and negative controls

Seed every MF-01…MF-10 witness; recover known symmetry/parity/holonomy counterexamples; verify no false witness under a strengthened premise.

## Expected complexity

Worst-case exponential or superexponential in state bound; symmetry reduction and SAT/SMT encoding are optional later engineering choices.

## Candidate/theorem falsification role

Any conjecture admitting a bounded exact counterexample, and any claimed minimality defeated by a smaller witness.

- **Models:** MF-01;MF-02;MF-03;MF-04;MF-05;MF-06;MF-07;MF-08;MF-09;MF-10
- **Theorem prospectuses:** TH-01;TH-02;TH-03;TH-04;TH-05;TH-06;TH-07;TH-08;TH-09;TH-10;TH-11;TH-12;TH-13;TH-14;TH-15;TH-16;TH-17;TH-18;TH-19;TH-20;TH-21;TH-22;TH-23;TH-24

## Lean/Python division

- **Lean kernel:** Consume exported witnesses as explicit finite examples and prove checker certificates, not trust the enumerator as an oracle.
- **Python reconnaissance/implementation:** Enumerate, reduce by isomorphism, and export candidate witnesses/counterexamples.

## Explicit nonclaim

Failure to find a bounded counterexample is not a proof of the unbounded conjecture.
