# TP-04 — Bridge-composition and confluence checker

**Status:** later toy-program contract; no code written in CST-EXP-05.
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## Purpose

Compare direct and factored bridges and enumerate replacement-order critical pairs.

## Input types

Finite carrier-stage graph; typed edge bridges; composition operation; retained object; local compatibility and clock maps.

## Exact outputs

Composable/not-composable verdict; composite relation/map; direct-versus-composite comparison; critical-pair joinability table.

## Witness and certificate format

Two explicit paths, their induced maps/relations, first disagreement state, and an unjoinable critical-pair diagram.

## Positive and negative controls

MF-02 concatenable corridors; MF-08 flat and nonflat variants; same bridge factored through an identity stage.

## Expected complexity

Polynomial for one declared pair of paths; potentially exponential when enumerating all paths/critical pairs in an unrestricted finite graph.

## Candidate/theorem falsification role

A functorial or transitive claim whose composite disagrees with the direct bridge or fails a typed boundary condition.

- **Models:** MF-02;MF-08
- **Theorem prospectuses:** TH-02;TH-03;TH-07;TH-18;TH-23

## Lean/Python division

- **Lean kernel:** Prove composition laws under explicit compatibility hypotheses and encode counterexamples otherwise.
- **Python reconnaissance/implementation:** Enumerate finite paths/critical pairs and emit disagreement certificates.

## Explicit nonclaim

Confluence of one retained object does not establish full package preservation.
