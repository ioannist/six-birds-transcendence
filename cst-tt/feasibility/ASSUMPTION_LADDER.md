# CST-EXP-05 Assumption Ladder

The ladder prevents structural mathematics, target realization, lineage, autonomy, recognition, ontology, and personal identity from doing one another's work. Refusing a higher rung never invalidates a lower structural theorem; it removes only the interpretation licensed by that rung.

## SF-01 — Integrated Autonomous Handoff

| Rung | Question | Exact role | Assumption IDs |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unconditional structural mathematics | Finite carriers, typed clause records, hypergraph gluing, ownership graphs, withdrawal, invariant sets, and exact counterexamples can be defined and proved without A1, A2-P, recognition, or identity premises. | `D-CARRIER` |
| 2 | Target package preservation | Requires a stipulated source package plus independently checked clause maps, target-local witnesses, hard gates, and H-GLOBAL-GLUE.  TH-05 is conditional on these structural premises. | `H-SOURCE-FULL;H-PRESERVE-O;H-GLOBAL-GLUE;H-TARGET-FULL` |
| 3 | Lineage rather than similarity | SF-01 supplies no lineage.  Add SF-02 or SF-03, or return S-L underdetermined. | `H-LINEAGE` |
| 4 | Target autonomy | Requires H-AUTONOMY cashed out by an explicit withdrawal set, target boundary, disturbance class, and invariant/horizon proof. | `H-AUTONOMY` |
| 5 | Certificate soundness and recognition | H-CERT-SOUND is needed to interpret the structural certificate as closure-complete; H-RECOG is needed for empirical attribution.  Neither repairs a failed structural gate. | `H-CERT-SOUND;H-RECOG` |
| 6 | A1 | A1 can only conditionally map lawful audited closure to ontological realization after structural and soundness premises.  It does not add lineage. | `A1` |
| 7 | A2 or narrower substitute | A2-P does not prove bridge existence.  A narrower theorem-specific substitute is H-XCI for the declared package object and bridge class. | `A2-P;H-XCI` |
| 8 | Phenomenal/survival language | Consciousness, survival, and numerical identity remain interpretive.  Personal identity additionally requires H-IDENTITY-O and a selected lineage family. | `H-IDENTITY-O` |

**Claim ceiling:** A passed structural package and autonomy audit do not establish source lineage, consciousness, personal survival, or numerical identity.

## Shared no-go kernel

The finite symmetry, public-shadow, parity, decoder-dependence, holonomy, scalar-residual, and local/global counterexamples do not require A1, A2-P, certificate soundness, empirical recognition, or H-IDENTITY-O. Those assumptions cannot rescue a failed antecedent or erase a finite countermodel.
