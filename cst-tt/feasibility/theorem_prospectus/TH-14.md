# TH-14 — Symmetric-duplication unique-selection impossibility

**Status:** conjecture-grade theorem prospectus; not proved.
**Kind:** no_go
**Families:** SF-02;SF-03;SF-01
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## 1. Setup

One source and two qualifying targets are exchanged by an automorphism σ that fixes all admissible source-relative evidence.  Selector f is invariant/equivariant under such automorphisms.

## 2. Conjecture / candidate statement

f cannot select exactly one target.  If f(A)=true and f(B)=false, applying σ reverses the values while invariance requires them unchanged.

## 3. Proof spine

One-line automorphism contradiction; optionally express as absence of an invariant singleton subset of a two-element orbit.
