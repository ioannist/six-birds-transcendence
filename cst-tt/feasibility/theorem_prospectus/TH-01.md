# TH-01 — Finite predictive quotient and bridge descent

**Status:** conjecture-grade theorem prospectus; not proved.
**Kind:** constructive
**Families:** SF-03;SF-01
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## 1. Setup

Let S and T be finite transition systems on distinct carrier tokens, Π a finite probe family with horizon H, ≡_S and ≡_T equality of all Π-traces, and R⊆X_S×X_T a typed bridge relation.

## 2. Conjecture / candidate statement

The predictive quotients X_S/≡_S and X_T/≡_T are finite and decidable.  R descends to a well-defined quotient relation iff it is saturated under ≡_S and ≡_T; if R also matches all admitted steps and probes, the descended relation is a simulation, and a bisimulation when both directions hold.

## 3. Proof spine

Construct trace signatures; prove equivalence; define quotient blocks by canonical signatures; show saturation is exactly representative independence; lift the step clauses blockwise.

## 4. Exact finite cases

MF-01 supplies a positive saturated bridge and a public-only collapsing counterexample.  MF-05 supplies an independent package predicate not determined by the quotient.
