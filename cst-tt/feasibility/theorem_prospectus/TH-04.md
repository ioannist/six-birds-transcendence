**Status:** conjecture-grade theorem prospectus; not proved.
**Kind:** constructive

## 1. Setup

Let clause scopes form a finite hypergraph with the running-intersection property.  Each scope has a nonempty finite local witness relation, and adjacent scopes agree under their shared restriction maps.

## 2. Conjecture / candidate statement

There exists a global witness whose restriction lies in every local relation.  If the family of restrictions is jointly injective along a join tree, the global witness is unique.  Dropping running intersection permits compatible-looking local records with no global witness.

## 3. Proof spine

Choose a join tree; extend a root witness leaf by leaf using overlap agreement; use running intersection to preserve earlier assignments; prove uniqueness by induction under joint injectivity.
