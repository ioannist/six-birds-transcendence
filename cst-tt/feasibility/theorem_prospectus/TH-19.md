# TH-19 — A small scalar residual can hide a catastrophic local defect

**Status:** conjecture-grade theorem prospectus; not proved.
**Kind:** no_go
**Families:** SF-01;SF-02;SF-03

## 1. Setup

Let scalar score be a positive weighted average of at least two defect coordinates, and let one coordinate have an independent hard threshold.

## 2. Conjecture / candidate statement

For any scalar tolerance τ>0, choose the critical coordinate's weight sufficiently small so the scalar score is below τ while the hard threshold is violated.

## 3. Proof spine

Set every noncritical defect to zero, the critical defect to one, and its scalar weight below τ.
