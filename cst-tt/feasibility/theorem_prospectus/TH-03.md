# TH-03 — Route independence from trivial cycle-basis holonomy

**Status:** conjecture-grade theorem prospectus; not proved.
**Kind:** constructive_boundary
**Families:** SF-01;SF-02;SF-03
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## 1. Setup

Let G be a connected finite route graph and every oriented edge carry an invertible map on a finite retained object O, with reverse edges interpreted by inverses.

## 2. Conjecture / candidate statement

Transport between any two vertices is path-independent iff the induced action of every fundamental cycle in one spanning-tree cycle basis is the identity on O.

## 3. Proof spine

Reduce any closed walk by tree cancellation to a product of fundamental cycles; prove cycle-basis identity implies all loops act trivially; obtain path equality by closing one path against the reverse of the other.  The converse is immediate.

## 4. Exact finite cases

MF-08 is the one-flip nonflat witness; its compensating-flip variant is the flat positive control.
