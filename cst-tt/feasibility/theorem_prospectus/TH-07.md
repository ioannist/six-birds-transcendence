# TH-07 — Composition of finite causal-overlap corridors

**Status:** conjecture-grade theorem prospectus; not proved.
**Kind:** constructive
**Families:** SF-02
**Phase:** CST-EXP-05

## 1. Setup

An overlap corridor is a finite support sequence with no empty process state, at least one nonredundant mixed-support update, signature preservation, and a target takeover boundary.  Two corridors share an exactly matched intermediate process frontier with no temporal gap.

## 2. Conjecture / candidate statement

Concatenation of the two corridors is an overlap corridor from the first source to the final target.  The induced lineage predicate is relation-valued and transitive on composable corridors; it rejects MF-03 because the mixed/no-gap antecedents fail.

## 3. Proof spine

Concatenate stage sequences; preserve no-gap and signature conditions; retain a mixed stage from each leg; discharge boundary ownership and clock compatibility.

## 4. Exact finite cases

MF-02 is positive.  MF-03 supplies endpoint equality and causal provenance without a live mixed corridor.
