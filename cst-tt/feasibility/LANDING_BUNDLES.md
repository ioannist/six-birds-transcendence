# Unranked Landing Bundles

These bundles are reconnaissance products, not a primary/fallback target choice. `LB-01`, `LB-02`, and `LB-03` remain unranked. `LB-NG` is a companion boundary kernel rather than a transfer candidate.

## LB-01 — Integrated target package plus withdrawal autonomy

- **Family:** SF-01
- **Constructive core:** TH-04;TH-05;TH-06
- **Negative/boundary core:** TH-17;TH-20
- **Models:** MF-07;MF-10
- **Toy contracts:** TP-07;TP-08;TP-10
- **Lean kernel:** Acyclic gluing; record assembly from independent obligations; controlled-invariant autonomy; explicit decoder-shadow counterexample.
- **Python support:** Clause/gluing audit, withdrawal reachability, and one-obligation-removed enumeration.
- **Landing shape:** A conditional structural theorem about target realization/autonomy, with lineage deliberately absent.
- **Principal risk:** Highest package-definition and nonvacuity risk; should be decomposed before any full theorem.
- **Kill condition:** No nontrivial MF-07-like positive model survives an independent clause and ownership audit.

## LB-02 — Finite overlap-corridor lineage

- **Family:** SF-02
- **Constructive core:** TH-07;TH-08
- **Negative/boundary core:** TH-14;TH-21;TH-22
- **Models:** MF-02;MF-03;MF-04
- **Toy contracts:** TP-03;TP-04;TP-10
- **Lean kernel:** Corridor definition; concatenation; branching relation; endpoint-equality countermodel.
- **Python support:** Event-graph corridor checker and branch classifier.
- **Landing shape:** A conservative relation-valued process-lineage theorem and exact exclusion of detached copies under that criterion.
- **Principal risk:** Definition may smuggle continuity or become too permissive/too strict.
- **Kill condition:** A bounded counterexample breaks corridor composition or a detached reconstruction passes every noncircular condition.

## LB-03 — Least relation-valued predictive ancestry

- **Family:** SF-03
- **Constructive core:** TH-01;TH-09;TH-10;TH-11
- **Negative/boundary core:** TH-13;TH-14;TH-15;TH-16
- **Models:** MF-01;MF-03;MF-04;MF-06
- **Toy contracts:** TP-01;TP-03;TP-06;TP-10
- **Lean kernel:** Predictive partitions; finite monotone closure; leastness; fission/fusion; independent-twin exclusion.
- **Python support:** Relation closure, derivation trees, quotient calculator, and finite counterexample search.
- **Landing shape:** A computable heterogeneous ancestry relation with explicit branching and no unique-identity claim.
- **Principal risk:** Operational seed may function as an unexamined ancestry oracle.
- **Kill condition:** No seed/closure law separates source-derived reconstruction from an independent twin without circular language.

## LB-NG — Shared finite no-go kernel

- **Negative/boundary core:** TH-13;TH-14;TH-16;TH-17;TH-18;TH-19;TH-20;TH-23

- **Landing shape:** Companion boundary results that remain valuable whichever constructive family is frozen in CST-EXP-06.
