## Likely contribution to CST exploration

The exposure theorem is central to distinguishing a genuine target realization from an exposed behavioral copy. It formalizes how making an internal determining variable fully visible can collapse the predictive distinction that supported the higher layer, while non-exposure preserves it. The associated current-versus-predictive quotient distinction can help specify hidden state that must be retained even when it cannot descend to the public interface.
