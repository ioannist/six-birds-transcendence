## Likely contribution to CST exploration

It distinguishes agenthood—the enablement and persistence of a maintained package—from agency—the within-layer causal capacity of its interface. Viability kernels, ledger-gated feasibility, feasible empowerment, packaging idempotence defect, null regimes, and the firewall between action and exogenous schedule are directly useful for testing whether a target package can maintain itself and causally act after handoff.

## Explicit boundary / what it cannot establish
