## Formalization and toy status

Manuscript reports exact finite witnesses and formal support for quotient/transport laws; cross-carrier typing is absent.

## Likely contribution to CST exploration

This is one of the most important continuity papers. It separates current and future-predictive quotients; proves continuation-induced transport on predictive classes; characterizes when transport descends to current classes; and uses split pairs and loop holonomy to show how a route can leave all current tests unchanged while changing future continuation. It is the natural formal basis for detecting path dependence, trauma-like changes, and route-sensitive identity across a transfer process.

## Explicit boundary / what it cannot establish

The results are on declared finite route packages and fixed support. They identify the right obstruction structure, but do not yet define or prove a cross-carrier subject-preserving transport.

## Distinct-carrier generalization required

Retype route interfaces over distinct carriers, prove predictive transport is well-defined across heterogeneous histories, and state whether lineage is relation-, branch-, or function-valued under duplication.
