\section{Reproducing the results}
\label{app:repro}

We designed every result to be rechecked from the repository. Lean 4.28.0
builds our development with its core library only and no external mathematics
library. Our validator then checks each registered result against its
declarations and confirms that only the standard axioms are used. Python
scripts check the transcription of the finite models, export the result
snapshot, and regenerate the evidence map. We give the commands, and the counts as of the release, in the artifact's reproducibility notes and in the data and
code availability statement.
