\section{The finite models}
\label{app:models}

We describe each model in words. Appendix~\ref{app:index} names the Lean
definitions, and the artifact contains the full data. Every computed value
below, such as a state count, a mass, a distance or a refinement trajectory,
is read from the result snapshot and pinned to a Lean theorem that states it.
The other small numbers, such as the numbers of stages, copies, bits and
programs, describe how a model is specified, and the Lean definitions fix
them.

\subsection{The two-copy deadline model}
\label{app:twocopy}

The base is the deadline system of \citet{TsiokosForcing2026}: a walker on a
wheel of positions with a hidden two-valued phase, a deadline, and a viability
kernel on which a predictive self-coordinate is forced. We take
two copies, tagged source and target. With the lethal states, the tagged
state space has \snapPosZeroFourTaggedAmbientStates{} states, of which
\snapPosZeroFourTaggedLethalStates{} are lethal. The handoff adds a position
along the episode, a token budget with a recharge owned by each tag, and a
register recording the route.

We run the episode through four stages: source live, two overlap stages in which
both copies are live, and target live. Two checked routes lead from distinct
source histories to the target.

The conditions hold as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Objecthood}, condition (1), is the deadline system's staged closure
kernel. At perturbation size \snapPosZeroFourCOneEpsilon{}, the perturbed
two-step kernel has a unique fixed point that keeps
\snapPosZeroFourCOneRecurrentMass{} of its mass on the specified four-state
core. Its total-variation distance from the uniform distribution on the core
is \snapPosZeroFourCOneTotalVariation{}.
\item \emph{Strict refinement} and the \emph{predictive self-coordinate},
conditions (2) and (3), are the deadline system's own, computed on each copy.
The refinement trajectory is \snapPosZeroFourCThreeTrajectory{}.
\item \emph{Viability}, condition (8), is the greatest funded kernel. It is
nonempty with recharge and empty without.
\item Conditions (4) to (7) are simplified. Non-exposure is replaced by a
single collision between two states that agree on the current channels and
differ in their predictive state. Action credit is replaced by a declared
three-cycle credit rule. Temporal closure is replaced by a declared order on
events, with a record and a tick added for the purpose. Binding is replaced by
a declared coupling between two assemblages.
\end{itemize}

The staged map carries all \snapPosZeroFourStagedClauseCount{} conditions from
source to target. A rational-register extension of this model carries the
robustness result of Proposition~\ref{prop:box}.

\subsection{The chain model}
\label{app:chain}

The chain model has a finite factor of \snapGSevenNativeFactorStates{} states,
three bits for phase, visibility and a hidden bit, together with unbounded
counters, ticks and histories. Its refinement trajectory is
\snapGSevenCThreeTrajectory{}.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The chain.} An episode passes through the systems
\snapGSevenStrictChainSystemIds{} by two handoffs.
\item \emph{The fork.} A symmetric fork hands off from the first system to
two targets, \snapGSevenForkSystemIds{} with the source listed first, and both
branches receive identical inputs.
\end{itemize}
Conditions (1), (2), (3) and (8) are computed from the model's own dynamics:
a nonuniform fixed point, a computed split, a recursive refinement, and a
greatest viable set that empties when recharge is cut. Conditions (4) to (7) are
simplified, as above. Disturbances form a unit class.

\subsection{The fusion model}
\label{app:fusion}

The fusion model has \snapFullNativeZeroTwoPhysicalStates{} physical states and
two installed programs, together with addresses, tokens of one unit each, and
accounts. Its viability factor, the physical state together with the charge,
has \snapFullNativeZeroTwoCEightFactorStates{} states. The operational core
kept under renewable supply has \snapFullNativeZeroTwoOperationalCoreStates{}
states. The refinement trajectory is \snapFullNativeZeroTwoCThreeTrajectory{}.

Its execution histories grow without bound, and we never enumerate them. All eight conditions are computed
from one installed execution, with the choices stated in
Section~\ref{sec:ancestry}. A guarded fusion has
\snapFullNativeZeroTwoFusionOutcomeCount{} settlement outcomes. We specified the model, and fixed its readings, before evaluating it.

\subsection{Small controls}
\label{app:controls}

For several countermodels we use small dedicated systems:
\begin{itemize}
\item a source-only and a target-only stage, for the detached copy
\item two reversible three-state route maps that do not commute, for holonomy
without an arrow;
\item a two-phase clock loop, for the absence of a canonical present
\item a singleton clock, for a tick without progress
\item pairs of defect vectors, for scalar summaries
\item a relabelled two-branch fork, for the symmetric fork
\item a countdown of length $h+2$ for each horizon $h$, for finite success
\item a domain of three one-event routes, for the licences
\item controls separating direct succession from overlap and from data
reachability.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The checks, expanded}
\label{app:checks}

The definitions in Sections~\ref{sec:checks} and~\ref{sec:continuation}
summarize the formal gates, and here we expand them component by component.
The named Lean predicates, listed in Appendix~\ref{app:index}, are
authoritative. This list explains them and does not replace them.

\paragraph{The strict handoff gate.}
We say that a handoff passes when all of the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Source and target are distinct, and there are at least three stages.
\item The entry stage's support is exactly the source and the exit stage's
support is exactly the target, with matching owners.
\item Every stage is realized, has nonempty support, has its owner in its
support, and has a nonempty maintenance state.
\item Some stage contains both systems.
\item Event identifiers are unique, and events have valid stages and owners.
\item Every dependency points forward in stage.
\item There is one readout update per adjacent pair of stages. Each update
changes the predictive field by its specified nonnegative increment, and
increases order, tick, progress and records by strictly positive amounts. It appends a nonempty extension to the
route memory, keeps a nonempty maintenance state, and keeps the owner in the
support.
\item An intervention check holds: either additive nonredundancy of the
projections, or failure of the episode once mixed support is removed. An
audit check also holds.
\item The route status is route-indexed.
\end{enumerate}
A chain of handoffs passes when each handoff passes, each pair of consecutive
handoffs agrees exactly at the join (the same intermediate system and the same
boundary stage, including its whole frontier, route memory and owner), and the
first source differs from the last target.

\paragraph{The continuation gate.}
We say that a handoff model with a source assignment passes the gate in its
realization-preserving version when all of the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Raw laws.} The recipe for the viability condition equals the fixed
rule, and the resource runtime's presence and requirement instructions agree
with the nominal recipe.
\item \emph{Protocol.} The withdrawal metadata decodes, and a finite check of
the decoded rows, policy, ticks, account record, routes and writers passes.
\item \emph{Readiness.} The acquisition and resource inputs are ready, and
any missing consumed input is rejected, not defaulted.
\item \emph{Domain.} The required domains are nonempty, and the disturbances,
actions, outcomes, factors and resources are each enumerated completely by
finite lists.
\item \emph{Source.} The source assignment satisfies the eight conditions on
the source.
\item \emph{Coherence.} The owner map is injective. The system and schema
checks pass. The temporal condition's rule is the temporal decoder. The
presentation is coherent, and so is the target's execution.
\item \emph{Realization.} For the mapped pieces, the cover, scope, totality,
reference and value-preservation checks pass, and every source cell is
covered. Every condition accepts its mapped piece on the target, and the
mapped pieces agree on every edge of the cover.
\item \emph{Lineage.} The complete lineage check passes, together with the
typed endpoint check. For every route the computed legs form a strict chain.
The seed, factor transport, record transport and retained route readouts pass.
The execution frontiers of both systems satisfy the semantic readout,
including sufficiency of the predictive code for every finite sequence of
actions within each system. The translation commutes with initial states,
transition weights, successors and observations on the whole reached set of
source states. Both frames share the runtime schema, and the execution links
pass.
\item \emph{Autonomy.} A game invariant certificate exists in the sense of
Lemma~\ref{lem:strategy}, with these instantiations:
\begin{itemize}
\item the entries are the registers reached by one step after a genuine
arrival;
\item the withdrawal removes all six declared roles, and keeps removed and
retained resources disjoint;
\item the realization predicate is realization together with membership in the
declared region and acceptance of the run's assignment;
\item the resource conditions require target ownership, retention and a
positive balance of every required resource, with the source absent and a
writer present;
\item legal moves are owned by the target, use only retained resources, and are
affordable;
\item the transition law requires resource closure, exact balance updates, and
strict advance of order, ticks, progress and records.
\end{itemize}
Responses are listed in a sparse format. At every generated play and for
every disturbance, every listed weight of the selected response is positive.
Unselected actions are not constrained.
\item \emph{Receiver and scope.} The receiver's content and intervention laws
hold, the endpoint is active, the route status is route-indexed, and
recognition is recorded as not addressed.
\end{enumerate}
The operational version is identical, except that the realization predicate in
the autonomy check is replaced by membership in the declared part of the state
space.

\paragraph{The finite autonomy certificate of Section~\ref{sec:autonomy}.}
We state this certificate for a finite controlled system. Its fields record the
withdrawals, and record that the boundary and the disturbance class were fixed
before evaluation. It also requires the following:
\begin{itemize}
\item the endpoint lies in the region;
\item realization holds in the region;
\item maintenance resources are retained and owned by the target;
\item for every state in the region and every disturbance, some
action owned by the target leads to a state in the region without stalling and within budget.
\end{itemize}
