## Raw model and size

The model has 6 source reachable history occurrences, 6 target controller states, 4 corridor events plus 3 declared prehistory events on each carrier (**10 event records total**), 3 corridor stages, 1 atomic leg, 2 theory routes, 2 branch assemblages per carrier (**4 total**), and **29 distinct clause-local coordinate keys**. Every target state carries its own C1–C8 local assignments (48 assignments), and both source route arrivals have C1–C8 assignments (16). Carriers 17 and 101 and the resource vocabulary follow FIACH-POS-01 conventions, but all dynamics, past events, clause objects, and predictions are **new declarations**. The one-leg corridor is the minimum permitted here; this fixture does not demonstrate nontrivial two-leg TTH-04 composition.

The distinct `A0a/A0b` histories are **not duplicate-state labels**. Each carrier declares a seed event and two different predecessor events. The left predecessor has prior health `safe` and native-maintenance provenance; the right has prior health `warning` and repair-after-hit provenance. The predecessor records lie **outside the audited temporal episode** and contribute zero to its tick/progress/record ledger; their different prior health and generator mechanisms converge to the same present temporal signature. This does not claim a hidden strict-record reset. The past event graph and source-to-target history map retain those different histories; the future interface begins at the present, so equal future behavior is an auditable prediction. `T-A0a` and `T-A0b` are distinct raw states with those different pasts; their common code is not state identity. The C4 witness is a **different** pair, `T-A0a/T-B0`: they share the public `report:safe` view but have different route-aware predictive futures under `hit` (warning versus safe health).

All target actions have cost 2, target-owned regeneration 2, and require the retained target regenerator; budgets are 2. The numerical rule is declared before M0b, not derived from an outcome. Deltas vary with state and disturbance, so future sufficiency cannot follow from uniform increments. Source dynamics mirror the declared finite transport frontier. Synthetic corridor intervention semantics start at `(0,0,0)` and add `(1,1,1)` for **each** mixed event; the two removal projections are derived by omitting the respective contribution. This is a **toy intervention rule**, not independent causal corroboration.

### C1 — declared surrogate

P05 requires “closure operators `E_τ` ... exact fixed-point and defect bookkeeping” and perturbation retention. The model declares two stagings: nominal-only and both disturbances. On the powerset of the six-state kernel, `E_τ(A)` is reflexive-transitive reachability from `A` through raw transitions allowed at staging `τ`. Check extensivity, monotonicity, idempotence, enumerate `E_τ(A)=A`, and compute for every `A` the exact symmetric-difference defect between `E_τ(A)` and one-step expansion `D_τ(A)=A∪succ_τ(A)`. Separately reconstruct the equiprobable nominal/hit Markov matrix `P` and the declared perturbation `P_ε=(1-ε)P+ε Uniform(K)` at `ε=1/16`; compute stationary fixed distributions and CORE retention against the preregistered `3/4` threshold. This borrows T1's [exact fixed-point/perturbation audit shape](../../six-birds-consciousness/THEOREMS.md#L14-L18), but **does not** reproduce T1's `P^τ C U` with canonical predictive-fiber prototypes or its ten-state exact facts. The local C1 coordinate also requires a nonempty `object_token`, package owner, and membership in the declared boundary, tied to the referenced closure audit. If any computed surrogate subcheck fails, report it; passing these is still not full C1. The raw negative deletes one transition, invalidating the declared total Markov row and changing closure reconstruction.

### C2 — substantive finite check

### C7 — substantive finite check

P05 T7 requires “two assemblages in one branchwise class and distinct recombination classes,” and names *bound-diagonal* and *bound-crossed* as its witness ([P05 T7](../../six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_An_Audited_Closure_Architecture_for_Consciousness_Exact_Finite_Models_Structural_Theorems_and_Conditional_Realization.tex#L1286-L1303)). MER defines the branchwise summary as weighted predictive-class content of independent members and an admissible recombination observable as an **exact finite event distribution on assemblages** ([MER quotient definitions](../../six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_Marking_Erasure_and_Recombination_on_Fixed_Support_Exact_Finite_Evidence_from_Six_Birds_Theory.tex#L145-L180)). We use the **same two-element assemblage domain** for both quotients on each carrier. The two assemblages contain literally identical *ordered* left records (`red,blue`) and ordered right records (`circle,square`), with equal unit weights and identical member predictive classes. Thus even retaining branch labels and order independently cannot distinguish them. Their raw joint-coupling edges differ: diagonal pairs red–circle and blue–square, crossed pairs red–square and blue–circle. The declared `jointPairing` observable is admissible because it acts on the **joint assemblage**, reads its coupling edges, and returns the normalized exact distribution of paired-content events (one count per edge, divided by the two edges). Its output is not reconstructible from either branch's complete local records: both local records are fixed while the coupling changes.

Formally, our `K_bw(a)=K_bw(b)` iff their complete ordered weighted branch-local predictive records agree. This is a **stronger, conservative finite control** than MER's exact branchwise quotient: MER forgets branch labels and aggregates weights by predictive class. We do not identify our `K_bw` with that exact quotient. `R_rec(a)=R_rec(b)` iff this finite `K_bw(a)=K_bw(b)` **and** every declared joint observable has the same distribution on them. The always available branchwise readout is included in the recombination interface; this makes `R_rec` refine the finite `K_bw` and gives the canonical forgetful map. The **same** C7 predicate checks raw assemblage/decomposition/reassembly well-formedness, two admissible bijective coupling maps as a proper subset of all four maps on two slots, and a witness `a≠b ∧ K_bw(a)=K_bw(b) ∧ R_rec(a)≠R_rec(b)`. Proper-subset coupling admissibility is an auxiliary finite constraint, **not** the strict-refinement witness. `bindingProbe(pair)=admit` iff reassembly has an image for that pair; it remains a derived display only, while `jointPairing` is the admissible joint observable. The controller's hit action queries the admitted left/right branch pair, which alone is not independent binding evidence.

The staged map sends each source branch-local record, each coupling edge, and each source assemblage (`diagonal→diagonal`, `crossed→crossed`) to its target counterpart. Its C7 check requires decomposition and reassembly commutation, `K_bw` and `R_rec` equivalence preservation **in both directions on all four ordered assemblage pairs**, and `mapDistribution(jointPairing_S(a))=jointPairing_T(mapAssemblage(a))` for each source assemblage. `NEG-C7-NO-JOINT-OBSERVABLE` is [T7's no-joint-observable aggregation control](../../six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_An_Audited_Closure_Architecture_for_Consciousness_Exact_Finite_Models_Structural_Theorems_and_Conditional_Realization.tex#L1298-L1304): delete the raw admissible joint-observable declarations on both carriers and their staged-map row, preserving both assemblage domains, all ordered branch records, coupling edges, admissible maps, and reassembly. The separate **system-level** `NEG-C7-UNCOUPLED` holds the observation interface fixed, including `jointPairing`, but gives both assemblages the same diagonal raw coupling on each carrier. This is a fixed-coupling finite control motivated by [MER's factorized `R=K` regime](../../six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_Marking_Erasure_and_Recombination_on_Fixed_Support_Exact_Finite_Evidence_from_Six_Birds_Theory.tex#L180-L183): no assemblage-dependent coupling remains for that observable to distinguish. Both controls predict `R_rec=K_bw` and no strict-refinement witness under the **unchanged** C7 predicate. `NEG-C7-PRESERVATION` changes only the target crossed assemblage's raw edges to diagonal edges; the unchanged transport equation must fail. M0b must separately report any local reassembly/coupling well-formedness failure for either edge mutation, especially `NEG-C7-PRESERVATION`; such a failure must not be presented as isolated transport evidence. None of these mutations changes the quotient rules or stipulates a verdict.
