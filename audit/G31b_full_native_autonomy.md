## Interface and shared execution

`Epoch.accept` (exported as `Package.Accept02`) is a computed Boolean:
authenticated charged access to canonical32, maintained inside state, valid
complete current-owner epoch replay, and a qualifying contiguous local block.
The replay reconstructs birth ROM/state/cursor/parent references from the actual
append-only event prefix. Every current-epoch ordinary record must match its
actual installed row, owner/issuer, key, cursor, serial, packet and predecessor.
Conditional paid fusion records cannot count as the fresh output's own cycle.
Current access separately authenticates installed ROM, resident owner and local
custody. Old parent records certify provenance, never newborn local credit.

A qualifying block is nonempty, consecutive, closed on the entire five-bit
physical state, supported by the current installed generator, locally issued
with actual renewable packets, and has unequal nonzero forward/reverse products.
All contiguous blocks are examined; there is no tuned tail length or threshold.
Once a genuine block is completed, later supported maintenance need not close
the entire growing audit tail again. The complete replay and current ownership
still authenticate every later step. A new birth starts a fresh evidence epoch.

The G27 `OriginalPackage` audit-tail dispatcher and the G15c impossibility at its
literal Frame interface are untouched. Accept02 is the **explicit designed
process-evidence successor** approved in G30, not a proof that old G27 admission
survives an odd phase flip. The rejected always-closed-audit/finite-full-history
readings in G30 remain rejected. Physical stationarity is a property of the
installed matrix; current history registers increase and are not stationary.

`WholePackage.Certificate` contains only theorem projections of one actual
