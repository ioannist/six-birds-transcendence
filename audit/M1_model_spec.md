## C1 CORE and robustness rule

The [T1 C1 catalog](../../six-birds-consciousness/THEOREMS.md#L12-L49) states that its closed-loop law uses `E_τ=P^τ C U`, that transit fibers are transient, and that at **τ=2**, **ε=1/16** the robustness criterion requires “a unique perturbed fixed point, at least 3/4 mass retained on CORE, nontriviality relative to the uniform law,” with exact defect bookkeeping at both perturbation values. [P05 C1](../../six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_An_Audited_Closure_Architecture_for_Consciousness_Exact_Finite_Models_Structural_Theorems_and_Conditional_Realization.tex#L567-L581) calls for declared multiscale closure operators, fixed points, defects, and perturbation thresholds. T1 supports a recurrent/transient audit and `P² C U`; it does **not** prescribe the route-anchored union selector below. T1's CORE is a specific object on its ten-state carrier. Our selector is a declared finite adaptation, not an imported theorem or a claim that the two carriers have identical C1 structure.

For each carrier, reconstruct the unperturbed equiprobable nominal/hit matrix `P` from the unchanged raw response table. Form its directed support graph. A **closed recurrent component** is a bottom strongly connected component with no positive-`P` edge leaving it. It is **maintained** when all its states lie in the declared C8 kernel and carry raw C1–C8 local assignments, and every declared response in that component is funded, remains in the component, has carrier-owned action and generator provenance, and uses retained carrier-owned resources. The anchor set is read from the *pre-existing* staged source/target route-arrival tables. `CORE` is the union of maintained closed recurrent components meeting at least one anchor. If none qualifies, CORE is empty and retention fails. Recompute this rule on every mutation; do not freeze the baseline state list. The union is closed under the positive support of `P` and is therefore fixed by the existing reachability closure at the full disturbance staging. The old powerset reachability audit remains a separate diagnostic; it is not relabeled T1's `E_τ`. **Discriminating-power limit:** because anchors occur on both route components, this rule can admit essentially every maintained recurrent state. The unchanged `3/4` test then chiefly measures mass lost to transient leakage. A pass would give limited evidence for a sharply selected object, even though it would satisfy this declared surrogate threshold.

Compute the fixed raw-field predictive quotient `q` as before. Its class indicator `C` and uniform-within-class prototype `U` are reconstructed from the six raw states; no class labels or prototypes are stored. Compute `E₂^0=P² C U` and `E₂^(1/16)=P_(1/16)² C U` over exact rationals. For each, record the exact distribution-level fixed-point solution space and `δ=1/2 max_z ||E²(z,-)-E(z,-)||₁`. At ε=`1/16`, require **one** fixed probability distribution, require it to differ from uniform on the six-state kernel, and test its mass on the newly computed CORE against the unchanged `3/4`. **Also retain POS-02's separate `P_ε` stationary CORE-mass threshold check** on the newly computed CORE; its result cannot substitute for the T1-style `E₂^ε` result. The full POS-03 C1 surrogate requires both retention checks and POS-02's existing powerset closure/fixed-set/one-step-defect bookkeeping. There is no new small-defect threshold.

`NEG-C1-UNMAINTAINED-RECURRENCE` changes a raw B-route action owner to 901, an owner already present in the model. Its transition graph and `E₂^ε` remain unchanged, but the affected recurrent component no longer meets the maintained rule. We predict **both** retained-mass checks on recomputed CORE will fall below `3/4`; the `E₂^ε` prediction is **low confidence and post hoc**, since the POS-02 `2/3` diagnostic concerned `P_ε`, not the newly required `E₂^ε`. The mutation also harms other ownership and viability laws; M1b must report them separately. A failure of either retention negative is an honest failed control, not permission to edit the threshold or selector.

This remains a **declared C1 surrogate**: the carrier is not T1's pinned ten-state deadline kernel; its `P`, `C`, `U`, and CORE are new, and the full P05 cross-clause coherence and promotion gates are absent. C2–C8 retain POS-02 definitions and limitations.
