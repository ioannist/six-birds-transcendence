# G17: full native package construction, preregistration only

The next construction attacks recovery items 1 and 2 directly: a full original
future interface and the C1-C8 package on the same native physical execution.
The fixed recipe is `audit/proposed_models/G17-full-native-package.model.json`.
It has not been evaluated. No theorem row, status or ceiling changes here.

The current candidate fails at two concrete hinges: its original predictor
merges four native states and its funded rows have mass eight at scale one.
The new recipe retains native coupling, makes normalization explicit, carries
the clock phase in the physical state, and computes refinement from public
observations and raw weighted transitions. A replica bit provides a genuine
nontrivial predictive fiber. Both branch laws and joint laws use the same rows.

P05 defines a finite carrier (lines 447-456), predictive equivalence by full
future behavior (473-484), and the original C1-C8 contract (567-605). The
legacy hazard-list predictor is not that full equivalence. Its declarations
and refutations stay intact. A new full behavioral interface is an explicit
class (b) mathematical successor, not an unchanged-schema proof. The eight
original observation roles must all be realized in the history lift.

The frozen record observation is the count of actual locally issued receipts;
the route observation is immutable rooted snapshot residue. Complete receipt
payloads remain in the history for provenance audits. The C3 equivalence is
contextual: owner, rooted residue, order and local record count match. A shared
root admits differing current replicas after positive maintenance transitions.
The physical directional cycle makes two traversals of the three-kernel cycle,
alternating phase, with fixed replica, and therefore has six actual edges.

The full physical C1 includes the clock phase and the actual funded account
sector, with fixed owner/token/program/route parameters justified by execution.
Separate environmental ports supply consumed energy packets. A missing packet
depletes the token after its already charged step; it cannot grant indefinite
continuation. Source/bridge withdrawal retains those environmental ports. This
is an open-system energy boundary, not a claim of closed perpetual funding.
Both successor transactions consume distinct tokens and port packets. C1 is
not merely the four-state kernel C1. An unbounded increasing
history counter is a different carrier. The recipe explicitly retains any
remaining requirement at that boundary, including a stronger exposure-closure
lift if necessary. No physical fixed point alone settles the history question.
