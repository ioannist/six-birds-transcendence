**Recommended architecture:** `SourceState = sourceTag × Z`, `TargetState = targetTag × Z`, with disjoint carrier tags and T1's exact internal ten-state kernel on each. The source initially has an active T1 C1/C2/C3 component; its *full* C1–C8 package remains to be built. Before the corridor, the target may be a *latent* T1-capable device, but the maintained target package is unavailable or unfunded. At the mixed stage, source history and a target-owned receiver jointly support activation and transfer of predictive/branch/maintenance state. After withdrawal, target resources and generators alone maintain the exact T1 closed loop. The handoff needs **three separate checks**: (1) removing the source input blocks activation; (2) two distinct reachable source histories in the same predictive class map to the same target predictive class (representative independence); and (3) with the latent target and receiver protocol held fixed, two reachable source histories in *different* predictive classes both activate the target and yield *different* endpoint classes, each exactly equal to the declared history-map image of its source class (content sensitivity). A raw history map must also preserve the pinned dynamics and ownership translation. Distinct tags and an isomorphic transition table do not themselves prove continuation; a source-controlled on/off trigger could activate a preloaded target copy while failing check (3). These are **design conditions**, not evaluated properties [CST-TT C0 obligations](C0_core_design.md), [P05 C-package carrier data, lines 569–604](../../six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_An_Audited_Closure_Architecture_for_Consciousness_Exact_Finite_Models_Structural_Theorems_and_Conditional_Realization.tex#L569-L604).

The T1 data already allow candidate *source* representative histories with real past differences and the same terminal `M` value: `p1w0→p2w1→p1w0` and `p1w1→p5w0→p1w0` are supported by the published transition laws, and `p1w0→p2w1` is a strict edge. Their admissibility in the **declared corridor frontier**, coverage of all relevant histories, and target map equations remain to be established; the T1 history generator uses exact finite support [T1-L1 lines 122–161, 174–209](../../six-birds-consciousness/lab/sixbirds_consciousness/labs/t1_l1_self_wheel.py#L122-L209). ## 4. Interaction risks and principled resolutions

1. **Exact C1 versus full-carrier augmentation.** Adding budget/clock/route bits to `Z` changes canonical fibers, transition rows, the perturbation's uniform law, and possibly CORE mass. Resolution: keep T1's *post-exit C1 object* on the exact ten-state kernel and put corridor events/history indices in a typed interface around it. If the full package carrier includes extra varying state, prove a new full-carrier C1 theorem; otherwise label C1 as factor-only and lower the package ceiling. No claim of `77/80` for a product follows from the factor calculation.
2. **Future sufficiency and route memory.** T1 `M` predicts the declared continuation law of safe/lethal support, not automatically every maintenance, provenance, tick, and route observable. Publishing those in the current interface may split all `M` fibers; omitting them after observing outcomes would be tuning. Resolution: declare the full TemporalSignature observation interface before POS-04 evaluation; compute the all-word partition by fixed-point refinement and demand a genuinely merged class. A full-state code earns no nontrivial C6 sufficiency. A failure demotes C6 rather than altering its interface post hoc.
3. **Public C4 versus internal route provenance.** If endpoint route/phase is made public now, `p1w0/p1w1` cease to be a T2 hidden pair. Resolution: distinguish current declared public channels from internal predictive/route state *in advance*, retain a future route-sensitive observable, and include an expanded-public-channel negative. Hiddenness is interface-relative, never absolute [T2 nonclaim, lines 245–250](../../six-birds-consciousness/THEOREMS.md#L245-L250).
4. **C8 funding versus pinned `P`.** A budget or action filter that removes T1 positive transitions destroys the imported C1 matrix. Resolution: let funding gate availability of the whole closed loop before/after withdrawal, while the positive funded regime leaves every canonical row and its exact probability unchanged. Compute the funded gfp and underfunded collapse from the same raw resource rules. No arbitrary stored `viable=true` value.
5. **TTH-03 bridge versus T1's public split.** Equal public history with different phase is *not* representative independence. Use genuinely different past histories with equal terminal predictive value, define the reachable frontier and one strict edge, then check successor/order/tick/progress/record/owner/route transport on all covered histories. If the bridge cannot preserve the extended temporal interface, restrict or demote the row explicitly.
6. **Copy versus handoff.** Two isomorphic tagged T1 objects can be independently fabricated. Require a mixed-support atomic corridor, no gap, source and target intervention counterfactuals, a source-to-target state/history map, and target-only viability after withdrawal. Keep three obligations distinct: **source-absent activation failure**; **same-class representative independence** for different reachable histories; and **content sensitivity** with one fixed latent target and receiver protocol, where two different source predictive classes both activate the target and produce different endpoint classes equal to their respective declared map images. A source-driven on/off trigger can satisfy the first obligation while failing the third. Derive all three from raw event/action/history data, not proof fields or a route-status label.
