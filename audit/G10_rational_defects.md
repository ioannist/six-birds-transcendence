# G10 — exact labeled rational defect semantics

This packet extends the Nat-only defect theory to exact rational components. Every acceptance result is derived from the component values. The scalar counterexample is constructed by embedding the already committed TTN-10 accepted and rejected vectors, with identical component labels, hard/soft types and tolerances. No equal-scalar witness is assumed. No new fixture, primitive or stored verdict is introduced.

The original TTS-02 exact statement is unchanged: “Acceptance under the exact defect semantics is componentwise: every hard-gate defect is zero and every soft defect lies below its own rational tolerance; acceptance is downward closed and not determined by a scalar summary.” The G0 contract adopts nonnegative raw defect/tolerance values. They are checked by the acceptance predicate; for the released controls they follow from the exact Nat-to-Rat embedding. Arbitrary signed rational input remains representable and is rejected if a defect or tolerance is negative.

This is class (b), new generalization and constructive coverage. Every old declaration and registry evidence item remains. The rational law uses all Rat values, not a bounded enumerated or integer-only substitute. Component labels are Nat identities; the released components receive their original positions from a recursive deterministic lift. Labels/gate types/tolerances are invariant in the componentwise NoWorse relation. Acceptance itself reads defect, gate type and own tolerance, with no scalar average.
