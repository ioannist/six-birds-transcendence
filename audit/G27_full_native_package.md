## Honest repair and limits

The repaired C1 statement includes stationary laws and exact audit data at **all
four** frozen closure cases, uniqueness exactly in the three valid cases, and
the fourth case's explicit two-law counterexample. The strengthening is an
honest complete audit, not the false statement that all four cases are unique.
No horizon, perturbation or physical primitive was changed. It does not
erase the old stronger gate or relabel its rejected endpoint as accepted.

This package is an operational, shared-raw realization of the original prose
design obligations at the G17 disclosed interfaces. G15c proves why it cannot
be called literal acceptance at unchanged G15a Frame/MemoryFrames: all Factors
are internal, the predictor only sees phase, the original eight-register law
merges the four kernel states, its original nonuniform C1 is impossible for
every certificate, and funded rows have mass8 at scale1. The positive package
uses the already preregistered behavioral quotient and normalization, and
preserves those kernel negatives inside its audit panel or explicit exports.
This contract-level repair needs no new raw model or preregistration stop.

Physical stationarity and actual temporal continuation are separate projections
of the same execution data. We prove no finite maintained history carrier and
an actual logged return obstruction; we do not assert a stationary probability
law on the unbounded history carrier. C8 invariance concerns G24's native
maintenance path domain, not arbitrary source-destruction or snapshot operations.
The assembled certificates are checkpoint results; the conjunction including
current closed-cycle self-credit is not claimed invariant under every action.
Self-review checks a next supported step whose cycle credit rejects while its
inside charged viability remains true.

The all-word domains are finite open-loop words with all retained successors.
G26 joint laws observe paired-step boundaries and include initial observations;
G22 branch laws observe after each individual action. Neither is a feedback
policy theorem or a general multiplication theorem after conditioning. No
unrestricted gluing of overlapping global claims is asserted. Exact original
TTN-02/14 predicates, upgraded POS-04 extension, restoration and TTF-04/05
adjudication remain subsequent work. Registry statuses/ceilings remain
23 proved, 14 checked finite countermodels, 2 open.
