# G6d resource and entry interpreter seam

## Proved boundary

This seam extracts resources and acquisition execution into polymorphic typed
cell/register interpreters. It **does not complete RawHandoff, G6d-3, or the
TTH-06 promotion gate**. TTH-06 stays conditional. The previous exact POS-04
AF/AO equivalences and controls are unchanged; equivalence with this new
component binding remains to be proved.

New modules under `Targets/GenericHandoff/`:

- `ResourceRuntime`: multiple resources; run-dependent required support,
  balances, prices, credits and independently declared positive increments.
  Six-role withdrawal and response ownership/solvency are interpreted from raw
  values. Used resources must belong to the declared retained set. Actual update
  equations check every paid balance, four counters, retained residue and the
  next resource state. No conservation or finite-context hypothesis is imposed.
  `soundness` reuses `StrategyGame.invariant_game_soundness`; `game_growth`
  covers every play generated by the strategy, every disturbance and every
  positive-support response branch.
- `RegisterInitialization`: initial coordinate instructions have an **empty
  register namespace**. `initial` is defined from the assignment and selector;
  no supplied candidate run or default register is available. Initializer
  locality and missing-input rejection are proved.
- `AcquisitionExecution`: source histories execute actual supported instructions;
  `history_terminal_defined` and `arrival_terminal_defined` determine the source
  terminal and receiver result. Activation checks all three availability inputs
  plus the declared mixed-support requirement. Route references, contiguity,
  realization, atomicity and positive clock increments are checked. The raw
  increment equation derives strictness (`path_step_strict`). Phase-2 entries
  include **every actual positive-support branch** of the normalized arrival
  row, including unsafe branches, with no package/resource/exit filtering.
- `RuntimeComponents`: one `execution` object supplies the temporal, resource
  and acquisition projections. `rows_shared`, `update_shared`,
  `temporal_rows_shared` and `temporal_update_shared` prove exact identity.
  Code/future/observation use the committed `RegisterFuture` definitions;
  maintenance lists actual declared accounts and owner reads a required account.
  `resource_owner_link` holds on every resource-safe run, beyond snapshots.
  `Entries` is the defined acquisition relation. `operational_soundness` uses
  these entries; `source_absent_no_operational_readout` proves their absence
  defeats the resource game.
- `ResourceRuntimeWitness`: a **component** nonemptiness proof, not a new FIACH
  model. The declared rule is one deterministic maintenance action, a paid and
  recharged resource, and unit event advancement (the G6 nominal maintenance
  pattern). Budget/cost/recharge/owner are parameters. Positivity, affordability
  and conservation produce an invariant certificate. Zero funding and capped
  records are rejected. Its one-element factor/full-kernel auxiliary invariant
  is intentionally elementary; it supplies no package or lineage evidence.

Source-history execution requires admitted actions/disturbances and actual
positive support, not source-autonomy normalization/safety. Arrival responses
are normalized separately. This keeps historical acquisition separate from
post-withdrawal support. Distinct carriers are likewise an enclosing
`CarrierCheck`, not an extra premise of target resource autonomy.

## Eddy's missing-view correction

`missing_entry_view` now rejects `∃ h : Ready raw a, Entry ... h ...`
from a missing-view read, with **no Ready premise**. The prior implication had
incompatible premises and was vacuous; it is withdrawn. This is a (b) theorem
repair for TTH-06 component evidence, not a registry change.

Guard sweep: initializer/dependency/row/program missing guards already conclude
`¬ Ready`; runtime empty-disturbance, source-presence, depleted-balance, writer,
price/owner, residue and counter guards assume at most static readiness, which
does not enforce their violated value laws. The realization/clock/activation
guards likewise do not assume PathCheck/Activated in their premises. No second
incompatible-premise guard was found in this seam.
