`Frame`/`MemoryFrames` interface. It proves that the proposed unchanged-native
upgrade cannot supply nonuniform C1. The separate labeled-exit and paired
observers in G15a/G15b remain correct at their stated interfaces.

the states called outside by the separate kernel profile. The original observer
therefore does not inherit that profile's exit identities. Its native predictor
depends only on phase; all four embedded kernel states have the same predictor.

counter, ticks, residue and funding equal, the entire eight-component snapshot

in equal context, equality of these laws is exactly equality of phase.

This is a weighted-law theorem: funded original rows sum to eight because the
existing schema divides by one. These rows are not probability distributions.
